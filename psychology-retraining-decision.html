Funding that applies

Only UNE offers a cheap Commonwealth-supported first stage; every other online conversion course is full fee

Online courses are what you can start in Perth and keep after a move to Melbourne. Campus programs in Perth (Murdoch, UWA, Curtin) would force a transfer or a restart.

Rule B has no time limit in its wording

UNE's 2027 handbook: "You have completed a Bachelor qualification (AQF Level 7 or overseas equivalent) in any discipline." It grants 72 credit points automatically and leaves 12 psychology units. UNE Handbook 2027

The ten-year risk sits in another document

UNE's general advanced-standing guideline says previous studies "must be less than 10 years old". Your 2016 degree is over that line at any 2027 start. No UNE page says whether it applies to Rule B. Get it in writing.

Your master's is not a backup for Rule B

Postgraduate degrees go to Rule A, the full 24-unit degree. If UNE refuses the 2016 B.E., the real fallback is UNE's Graduate Diploma (no recency rule, about $22,531 CSP) or ECU Online.

Stage 1: the three-year sequence

CSP rates: psychology units in an approved bachelor-level "professional pathway" course are Band 2, $9,880 a full-time year in 2027 ($1,235 a unit). Graduate diploma units are Band 4, $18,025 ($2,253 a unit). If UNE's units were charged at Band 4, Rule B would cost about $27,000. Sources: UNE GDPSY, ECU Online, Newcastle 2027 fees, Deakin H603, APAC register (UNE).

Stage 2: fourth year, where marks decide everything

Late results cost a year

Most February fourth years close October to December. UNE Honours runs in T1 only, closes 31 October, and needs the whole sequence finished before you apply.

Deakin's sequence finishes too late for February

A full-time 2027 start ends T3 with assessment to 11 Feb 2028; results around 24 Feb 2028 (plausible, not published). Only mid-year fourth years remain: Deakin H650 (July), Federation (late July), and the six-intake online diplomas.

Rule for a working student

Finish the sequence by the end of a year's second study period, so results are in by about early December, if you want the next February honours round.

Stage 3: the fifth year, the hardest gate

The APS says the sector has room for about 10% of postgraduate applicants (APS, Sept 2024). A cheap campus CSP place that forces a year off work costs far more overall than a full-fee online one that keeps you employed.

Sources: Curtin MPP, UNE MPP, Cairnmillar MPP, Federation MPP, Swinburne clinical, PsyBA provisional registration. Provisional registration needs actual enrolment in the fifth year; an offer letter is not enough.

    Before you count on a full diary

    Client turnover is high

    With most clients capped at 10 rebated sessions, 16 sessions a week over 44 weeks is about 70 clients a year: more than one new referral every week.

    Referrals run through GPs

    Since 1 Nov 2025, Medicare referrals must come from the client's usual GP or MyMedicare practice. Telehealth means competing nationally on price. Health Department

    Demand is real, burnout too

    The April 2026 government study found a 57.3% shortfall in access in 2025. A 2022 survey found 26.4% of psychologists burned out. No data on telehealth saturation was found.

    Your Cert IV can pay $45 to $63 an hour casual now; paid psychology work starts in 2030 at the earliest

    First confirm the Cert IV is awarded: your placement is outstanding, and ads for these roles ask for the qualification. Peer worker roles also need lived experience.

    SCHADS award rates from 1 July 2026

    Ads: Perth casual support from $46.70/h with a Cert IV; Neami Melbourne $46.74–$50.11. One 8-hour casual Saturday a week for 48 weeks earns about $24,300 (Level 2.1) to $27,200 (Level 3.1). The award's pay structure changes on 1 Oct 2027.

    Paid psychology work, from provisional registration

    Provisional psychologists can work in paid, supervised psychological roles, including telehealth, but cannot bill Medicare. That is 2030 in Plans B and C, 2032 in Plan A.

    The degree will not tell you whether you enjoy client work; these will

    The first three or four years are research-heavy academic psychology: two research-methods and statistics units, then a thesis that is half the fourth year. Counselling-practice units arrive only in year four.

    Parallel Pakistani online study will not shorten your pathway

    A Pakistani BS Psychology is a four-year program; the Australian graduate route to the end of fourth year is about two years full-time or three to four part-time. Under Rule B your 12 remaining units are all core psychology, so you would do the same work twice.

    Take the tendering job now; retrain only if a test year confirms you want client work

    If the goal is less site work and more flexibility

    Switchgear tendering, estimating and proposals deliver it within months: $110k–$150k entry ($123k–$168k with super), senior roles $170k–$200k, often hybrid, paid leave kept, no retraining cost. Fully remote is rare. A predictable office role is also a far better base for evening study than commissioning.

    If you genuinely love client work

    Retraining is defensible, but it is not a money move. You pay $122k–$261k and five to eight years of 55–65 hour weeks, pass a gate that admits about one in ten, and land at about today's pay unless an own practice reaches 16+ completed sessions a week. Plan C is the best balance for someone who will not quit early; Plan A if money is tight and time is not.

    Registration rules in brief

    Provisional registration lasts 12 months, renewable twice; $0 to apply, $493 a year. General registration costs $604 plus $493 a year; the National Psychology Exam is $450, sat in Feb, May, Aug or Nov, pass mark 70%. The internship needs 1,500 hours including 500 client contact, 80 supervision (50 one-on-one) and 60 training, over at least 44 weeks full-time; part-time has no maximum. Higher-degree graduates do not sit the exam. The old 4+2 pathway closed to new entrants on 30 June 2022. In September 2026 the Board said it will recommend a single five-year degree leading straight to general registration, with no internship, exam or provisional registration; it needs government approval, has no transition rules yet, and current rules still apply. Recheck before applying for any fifth year. PsyBA fees, reform update.
