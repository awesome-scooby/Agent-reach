# Switch to tendering now, test psychology cheaply

Retraining as a psychologist from Perth can be done, and you can carry it on if you move to Melbourne. But it is a **5 to 8 year detour costing roughly $120,000 to $260,000 in today's dollars**, and it ends at about the income you earn now. So it is worth doing only if you genuinely want client work, not as a way to earn more or work less. Your 38-hour job survives the first four years, which are all online. It strains at the fifth-year placement: UNE's online master's needs **3 days a week for a whole trimester**. It breaks completely at the **1,500-hour internship**, because only psychological work counts towards it ([PsyBA 5+1 guidelines](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)).

**The three plans:**

| Plan | Route | Supervised work (provisional registration) | Independent practice (general registration) | All-in cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: cheapest | UNE Bachelor of Psychological Science under Rule B (12 units, about **$14,820 CSP** at 2027 rates), then UNE Honours (about $9,880 CSP), then a part-time online master's, then the internship | 2032 | **about late 2034** | about $122k to $141k |
| B: fastest | ECU Online graduate diploma from January 2027, then an online fourth year from mid-2028, then a full-time master's in 2030 | **February to March 2030** | **about late 2031** | about $216k to $261k |
| C: best while keeping the job | The same ECU online first four years, then UNE's part-time online master's | mid-2030 | 2032 to 2033 | about $155k to $177k |

**Income:**
- **Your $200 × 70% × 18 × 46 = $115,920 is about $30,000 below your real engineering package of about $126,148.** That gap appears once you count 44 working weeks, 10% no-shows, practice costs and super.
- Employed psychologists in WA schools earn about **$122k to $154k including super**.
- Solo telehealth at $200 beats engineering only once you sustain about **15 to 16 completed sessions a week**.

**Switchgear tendering, estimating and proposals** already pay **$110k to $150k** (senior roles $170k to $200k), often hybrid. That meets your immediate goal of less site work within months.

**My recommendation:**
1. Move into tendering in 2027.
2. Spend a few thousand dollars testing psychology: finish the Cert IV placement, do weekend support shifts, and take two UNE units.
3. Decide by **31 October 2027** whether to continue.

## 1. Options compared: plenty of online routes, but only UNE offers a cheap CSP first stage

### How the pathway works

Registration needs six years of training:
- **Years 1 to 3:** an APAC-accredited three-year sequence ("Level 1").
- **Year 4:** an accredited fourth year ("Level 2").
- **Years 5 to 6, either:**
  - a one-year fifth year, usually a **Master of Professional Psychology (MPP)**, followed by a **1,500-hour "5+1" internship**; or
  - a **two-year master's** (Level 3+4) that leads straight to general registration.

Sources: [PsyBA general registration standard](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33836&dbid=AP&chksum=%2fewcsB%2bopxW0wQs78bCldw%3d%3d); [PsyBA combined-program policy](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD20%2f30289&dbid=AP&chksum=aq2ZjYjsMu1I%2bO28g64lGg%3d%3d).

**Provisional registration** is the licence to do paid, supervised psychology work. It starts only when you have **finished the fourth year and enrolled in the fifth**. Ahpra wants the fourth-year results plus proof of fifth-year enrolment sent by the university ([PsyBA provisional registration](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional.aspx)).

Because you may move in 2027, the online options below matter most: they carry across the move unchanged. Campus programs in Perth (Murdoch, UWA, Curtin) would force a transfer or a restart.

### First stage (three-year sequence): UNE Rule B costs about $14,820; online graduate diplomas cost $26k to $45k

**What Rule B says.** UNE's 2027 handbook states: **"Rule B: You have completed a Bachelor qualification (AQF Level 7 or overseas equivalent) in any discipline."** Admission under Rule B brings **72 credit points of automatic block credit** and leaves **12 psychology units**: 10 core units plus 2 listed electives ([UNE Handbook 2027, BPSYSC](https://handbook.une.edu.au/courses/2027/BPSYSC)). The wording has been identical since 2023 and contains no time limit.

**The ten-year risk.** The risk sits in a different document. UNE's general advanced-standing guidelines say "previous studies must be less than 10 years old" ([UNE Advanced Standing Guidelines](https://www.une.edu.au/study/applying-to-une/transfer-and-advanced-standing/advanced-standing-application-guidelines), search snippet only). A 2016 degree is more than ten years old at any 2027 start.
- The handbook ties Rule B credit to the admission rule itself, which argues against the general guideline applying.
- No UNE document says so outright. **This is the one question to get answered in writing before you pay.**

**Your master's is not a backup.** If UNE says no, the 2020 La Trobe master's cannot replace the bachelor's for Rule B. The handbook sends "Postgraduate degrees" to Rule A, which is the full 24-unit degree ([UNE Handbook 2027, BPSYSC](https://handbook.une.edu.au/courses/2027/BPSYSC)). The real fallback is UNE's Graduate Diploma: its admission rule has no recency condition ([UNE Handbook 2027, GDPSY](https://handbook.une.edu.au/courses/2027/GDPSY)).

**Why Rule B is cheaper.** The price depends on the Commonwealth funding band, not on the course name.

## 2. Three plans: supervised work starts in 2030 at the earliest, independent practice in 2031 to 2034

### The late-results trap and UNE's once-a-year honours intake

Late results are the most common way a working student loses a year.

**When results arrive:**
- **Deakin:** a student who starts the H603 diploma full-time in T1 2027 finishes T3 with end-of-unit assessment running to **11 February 2028** ([Deakin 2027 calendar](https://leadership.deakin.edu.au/sites/default/files/2027-trimester-academic-calendar.pdf)). Deakin's usual lag is about two weeks, so results would land around **24 February 2028**. That date is plausible but not published.
- **UNE:** the latest release for T3 2027 results is **14 February 2028** ([UNE principal dates](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/principal-dates/principal-dates-2027), snippet).

**When applications close.** Almost every fourth year starting in February closes between October and December and needs results by then:
- **UNE** requires the whole sequence to be finished before its **31 October** close. Anyone "studying these units after the application closing date" is ineligible. UNE Honours runs in **Trimester 1 only** ([UNE Handbook 2027, HBPSYC](https://handbook.une.edu.au/courses/2027/HBPSYC); [UNE application process](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/psychology-fourth-year-program/honours-psychology-application)).
- **Deakin H452** closed on 18 November 2026 for 2027 entry ([Deakin H452](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/bachelor-psychological-science-honours)).

What someone whose last results arrive in late February 2028 can still start:

|---|---|---|
| UNE Honours | No | None (T1 only), so Feb 2029 |
| Deakin H452 | Unlikely | None found, so Feb 2029 |
| Deakin H650 | Unlikely | **T2 (July) 2028** |
| Online diplomas with six intakes (ECU, Monash, UTS) | March possibly; May realistic | **May, July, August** |

| Murdoch (Perth campus) | No | Semester 2 |

Two rules follow from this:
1. Finish the sequence by the end of the second study period of a year (results by about early December) if you want the next February's honours round.
2. Any sequence unit studied in UNE's T3 of the year you apply pushes UNE Honours back a full year. T3 starts in mid-October, before the 31 October close.

### Plan A, cheapest: the all-CSP UNE route, independent practice about late 2034

**The route:** UNE Rule B part-time from T1 2027, then UNE Honours part-time from T1 2030, then a fifth year that keeps you employed, then the internship.

**How the UNE schedule works.** UNE's official part-time plan has 2 units in each of T1 and T2 and nothing in T3 ([UNE Rule B part-time plan](https://handbook.une.edu.au/studyplan/e441b7f98314d3100646fc88beaad37a)). Two units take about **24 to 30 study hours a week** on top of work. UNE's own estimate is 12 to 15 hours per unit per week ([UNE Handbook 2027](https://handbook.une.edu.au/courses/2027/BPSYSC)).

**Can you go faster by adding T3 units?**
- Using the listed electives offered only in T3 (PSYC315 in T3 2027, PSYC309 in T3 2028) finishes the sequence **in T1 2029** instead of T2 2029 ([PSYC315](https://handbook.une.edu.au/units/2027/PSYC315); [PSYC309](https://handbook.une.edu.au/units/2027/PSYC309)).
- **But Honours still starts in T1 2030.** Applications close on 31 October 2029 either way. The gain is only that your results are in hand, rather than possibly pending, at that deadline.
- **Reaching Honours in T1 2029 needs one four-unit trimester.** The prerequisites make this unavoidable: all four 200-level units must be passed before the 300-level units, and 200-level units are not offered in T3. The only workable schedule takes all four 200-level units in T2 2027 (21 June to 17 September). That is a full-time study load of about 48 to 60 hours a week, which the official full-time plan itself uses ([UNE Rule B full-time plan](https://handbook.une.edu.au/studyplan/2041b7f98314d3100646fc88beaad371)).

## 3. Parallel Pakistani online study will not shorten the pathway

**It is very unlikely to save time or money, and it adds risk.**

**Why it won't shorten anything:**
- Registration and every accredited course are built on *APAC-accredited* sequences. Overseas study counts only after someone judges it "comparable" or "substantially equivalent".
- A Pakistani BS Psychology is a **four-year** program. AIOU launched one in 2025 as a four-year distance degree ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/07/16/aiou-introduces-three-new-bs-programs/amp/)). The Australian graduate route to the end of the fourth year is about two years full-time, or three to four part-time.
- Under UNE Rule B, the 12 units you have left are all core psychology. Doing similar units in Pakistan means doing the same work twice.

**Three different bodies decide three different things:**

| Decision | Who decides | What the rules say |
|---|---|---|
| Credit inside an Australian course | **The Australian university's credit or advanced-standing office**, under its own policy | UTS credits only "equivalent subjects from an APAC-accredited Level 1 program… within the last 10 years", up to 2 introductory subjects ([UTS](https://studyonline.uts.edu.au/online-courses/graduate-diploma-psychology)). Cairnmillar caps credit at 50%, wants an APS-assessed course, and will not normally assess domestic applicants with overseas qualifications before an offer ([Cairnmillar RPL policy](https://www.cairnmillar.org.au/about/policies/advanced-standing-and-recognition-of-prior-learning-policy/)). Deakin grants no credit from non-APAC courses |
| Comparability, for entry to accredited postgraduate courses | **The APS** (Australian Psychological Society) | Outcomes are only "three-year" or "four-year" comparability. Four-year needs an independent research project. "Final decisions… are made by individual universities" ([APS assessment](https://psychology.org.au/about-us/what-we-do/assessments/assessment-overseas-qualifications/australian-university-postgraduate-course)) |
| Substantial equivalence, for registration | **PsyBA/Ahpra**, at your registration application | Only UK, Irish and US accredited degrees are pre-recognised. The overseas assessment costs **$718, non-refundable** ([PsyBA overseas](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Overseas-Applicants/registration-requirements.aspx); [PsyBA fees](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Fees.aspx)) |

Two further limits: only **onshore** placement hours count ([APAC Evidence Guide](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Evidence-Guide-v1.4-final.pdf)), and the Cert IV in Mental Health earns no credit into accredited psychology units.

**Get these in writing before paying a Pakistani provider anything:**
1. A formal credit assessment from the specific Australian university, listing each unit credited and the course version.
2. An APS comparability outcome for that exact program and transcript.
3. If you want certainty about registration, an Ahpra assessment. In practice that is only available through a formal application with the $718 fee.

## 6. Recommendation: take a tendering job now, run a cheap psychology test, decide in October 2027

### If you genuinely enjoy client work, retraining is defensible, but it is not a money move

At best, psychology roughly matches engineering pay:
- A newly registered psychologist employed in a WA school or a private practice earns about the same package as you do now.
- Only an established solo practice at about 16 or more completed sessions a week clearly beats engineering.

**What you pay to get there:**
- $122k to $261k of cost.
- Five to eight years of 55 to 65-hour weeks.
- A fifth-year selection gate that admits about one in ten applicants.

**The verdict:**
- If client work is your calling, that price can be worth paying. **Plan C** is the best balance for someone who refuses to quit early, and **Plan A** is the best if money is tight and time is not.
- If what you actually want is **less site work and more flexibility**, switchgear tendering, estimating and proposals deliver that within months, at $110k to $150k, often hybrid, with no retraining cost.

**The two goals fit together.** A predictable office-based tendering role is a much better base for evening study than commissioning work with site travel. The tendering move is the right first step whichever way you decide on psychology.
