01

What moved since the baseline

8 Sep → 10 Nov 2026

Two months of real data. The central thesis — that Perth was at a cycle top and Melbourne near a bottom — is tracking, but rates moved against you.

RBA cash rate 4.85% was 4.35% · +50 bp

Hiked 3 Nov in a 6–3 split vote — the fifth rise of 2026 and the highest rate in 18 years. The "raise further if needed" line was dropped, so a pause is now more likely. Next decision 8 Dec.

Perth median dwelling $942,018 was $999,987 · −5.8%

Down 1.7% in October alone and −5.7% over the quarter. Annual growth has collapsed from +15.6% to +3.9%. The correction called in the model has arrived, and faster than modelled.

Melbourne median dwelling $773,027 was $786,718 · −1.7%

Falling, but 3.3× more slowly than Perth. Annual change −7.6%, so Melbourne is deeper into its own correction and closer to the other side of it.

Perth premium over Melbourne 21.9% was 27.1% · −5.2 pts

The gap is closing from the Perth side — exactly the direction the model predicted, but it means Perth is getting relatively cheaper. Worth watching: this is the main mechanism that could eventually favour Perth.

The rate rise costs you about $2,300 a year — and it is not priced into the main report

A 4.85% cash rate implies roughly 6.95% on a competitive owner-occupier variable, against the 6.35% used throughout the model. On a $480,000 loan that is $3,177/month instead of $2,987 (+$191/mo, +$2,287/yr), lifting repayments from 22.0% to 23.4% of net household income. Still comfortable on two incomes. Not comfortable on one — the parental-leave stress case now reaches roughly 47% rather than 44.5%.

Net effect on the decision: mildly positive for Melbourne. Perth has given back nearly 6% of value in two months while Melbourne gave back 1.7%. That is the thesis working. But higher rates reduce your borrowing capacity and raise the cost of waiting, so the case for acting once a job is secured has strengthened rather than weakened.

05

Where each number comes from

~10 min to do manually

Cash rate

RBA media release. Board meets roughly every six weeks; next 8 Dec 2026.

rba.gov.au/media-releases

Property values

Cotality Home Value Index, released the 1st of each month. Use the capital-city table.

cotality.com/au/insights

Perth medians & suburbs

REIWA publishes Perth metro and suburb-level data weekly.

reiwa.com.au/the-wa-market

Unemployment

ABS Labour Force, monthly. Use the state table, not the national headline.

abs.gov.au — Labour Force

Rents & vacancy

Domain Rental Report (quarterly) and SQM Research vacancy rates (monthly).

sqmresearch.com.au

Mortgage rates

Canstar or your own broker. The number that matters is what you can actually get, not the average.

canstar.com.au/home-loans

Melbourne suburb prices

Domain or realestate.com.au suburb profiles for Werribee, Melton, Tarneit.

domain.com.au — Werribee

Job market — the real one

Your own application count. No published statistic substitutes for how many offers Tiger is actually getting.

seek.com.au — Melbourne roles
If you only check one thing each week, check trigger 1. Property medians move slowly and a week of movement is almost always noise. Tiger's application count is the only indicator that is both fast-moving and decisive — and it is the only one entirely within your control.
