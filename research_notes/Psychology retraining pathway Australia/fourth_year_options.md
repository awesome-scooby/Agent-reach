# APAC-accredited fourth-year (Level 2) psychology options for a working graduate in Perth (possibly Melbourne from 2027), as of 25 November 2026

Research date: 25 November 2026. All dollar figures are AUD. "Verified" means I read the official page directly. "Via search extract" means the official site blocked direct fetching (HTTP 403: deakin.edu.au, monash.edu, une.edu.au, csu.edu.au, cqu.edu.au, utas.edu.au), so the figure comes from the search engine's extract of that official page and should be re-checked. Reddit could not be reached (the domain is blocked to the research tools), so **this file contains no forum anecdotes**. Every competitiveness figure below comes from an official university source.

Abbreviations: GDPA = Graduate Diploma of Psychology (Advanced). CSP = Commonwealth supported place. FEE-HELP = government loan for full-fee places. "Level 1" = APAC three-year sequence; "Level 2" = APAC fourth year.

## 1. Checking the applicant's Deakin figure (online honours about $9,900 CSP; 75% external average plus research methods)

### Takeaway
The figure is broadly correct. Deakin's **Bachelor of Psychological Science (Honours), course code H452**, is offered online and is CSP-funded: $9,537 indicative for 2026, and the 2027 government maximum for "Professional Pathway Psychology" is $9,880 per full-time year. External applicants need a **75% average over all level-2 and level-3 psychology core units plus at least 65% in a level-3 research methods unit**. Two points the applicant may not know: (a) external applicants compete for a **quota of 20 places**; (b) students who complete Deakin's own three-year bridging course (H603 Graduate Diploma of Psychological Science) count as **internal applicants**, with a 65% minimum average. A separate Deakin course, the **H650 GDPA**, is full-fee (about $34,400 for 2027) with a 65% minimum, but its recent cut-offs have been 72% or higher.

### Cited Findings
- H452 Bachelor of Psychological Science (Honours) is offered at "Burwood (Melbourne), Waurn Ponds (Geelong), and Online"; 1 year full-time or part-time equivalent; "Indicative CSP annual fee of $9,537 for one year full-time, commencing 2026"; 8 credit points; about 35 hours per week full-time; no residential or on-campus requirement stated (the annual Fourth Year Conference can be attended online); part-time students must complete the coursework in year 1; work experience and mandatory checks are not required (2026 handbook, verified) — [Deakin Handbook H452 (2026)](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H452&stutype=local)
- H452 structure: Trimester 1: HPS431 Principles of Psychological Assessment, HPS432 Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, HPS435 Research Project A. Trimester 2: HPS433 Applied Counselling Skills (Advanced), HPS434 Psychology Research Methods (Advanced), HPS436 Research Project B. Coursework is 50% of the honours grade and the thesis is 50%. The thesis package is a 1,500–2,000-word literature review draft, a 10-minute proposal presentation, a poster, and a 5,500–6,000-word thesis (4 credit points) — [Deakin Handbook H452 (2026)](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H452&stutype=local)
- Deakin admission schedule (policy document, version 27), H452:
  - **Internal applicants:** a Deakin bachelor degree with a psychology major, **or a "Graduate Diploma in Psychological Science or Graduate Diploma of Psychological Studies from Deakin University"**, with "a minimum of a mid-credit (65%) in Research Methods B and a minimum average of 65% over all level-2 and level-3 Psychology core units."
  - **External applicants:** an APAC three-year major or a Foundation Level 1 course "(for example: a three year bridging course – Graduate Diploma of Psychology) with a minimum of a mid-credit (65%) in level three psychology Research Methods unit and a minimum average of 75% over all level-2 and level-3 Psychology core units. **There is a quota of 20 places available for external applicants.**"
  - Source: [Deakin Schedule A – Minimum admission criteria (policy v27)](https://policy.deakin.edu.au/download.php?associated=&id=629&version=27)
- H452 ranking: "Applicants will be ranked on the basis of the marks achieved in the level-2 and level-3 HPS core units completed at Deakin University." Meeting the minimum does not guarantee an offer. An Indigenous entry stream requires a 65% average (2027 entry page, via search extract) — [Deakin H452 course page](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/bachelor-psychological-science-honours); [Deakin Handbook H452](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H452&stutype=local)
- H452 dates: "Trimester 1 2027 applications close 18 November 2026". The international listing shows "Trimester 1 only". I found no Trimester 2 intake for H452 (via search extract) — [Deakin H452 course page](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/bachelor-psychological-science-honours); [Deakin H452 international](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/bachelor-psychological-science-honours-international)
- The 2027 maximum student contribution for "Professional Pathway Psychology" (funding cluster 2) is **$9,880 per EFTSL** (one full-time study year) — [Study Assist – Student contribution amounts](https://www.studyassist.gov.au/financial-and-study-support/commonwealth-supported-places/student-contribution-amounts)
- H650 GDPA (Deakin), 2026 handbook (verified): Burwood, Waurn Ponds and Online. "Online is for domestic students only, with limited places." 1 year full-time or part-time equivalent, with coursework completed in year 1 for part-timers. **$32,800 for one year full-time (full-fee paying place); Commonwealth supported places aren't available.** Thesis: HPY720 Research Project A (Trimester 1) and HPY721 Research Project B (Trimester 2), 50% of the grade — [Deakin Handbook H650 (2026)](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H650&stutype=local)
- H650, 2027 course page (via search extract): entry requires 65% in a level-3 research methods unit and a 65% average over level-2 and level-3 psychology core units. "the mark 'cut-off' required for entry to Graduate Diploma in recent years has been at or above 72%". Domestic intakes are Trimesters 1 and 2. About $34,400 for one year full-time (full fee, 2027). Psychology completed more than 10 years ago requires evidence of currency — [Deakin H650 course page](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/graduate-diploma-psychology-advanced)

### Inferences
- If the applicant does the three-year sequence at **Deakin (H603 Graduate Diploma of Psychological Science)**, the policy wording puts them in the **internal** pool for H452: 65% minimum and outside the 20-place external quota. If they do it at UNE, Newcastle or ECU, they are external: 75% minimum, competing for about 20 places. Since ranking uses marks in Deakin HPS core units, Deakin graduates appear to have a structural advantage at H452.
- The roughly $9,900 figure matches the 2027 CSP rate ($9,880). A 2028 start will be indexed upward. I found no published 2028 rate, so roughly $10,000–10,300 is my own estimate.
- H650 is a fallback for applicants who miss H452: lower minimum, but about 3.5 times the cost and still competitive (72% or higher in recent years).

### Gaps
- Whether Deakin gives internal H452 applicants a conditional offer when their final trimester results are not yet released (for example, H603 students finishing in Trimester 3) is not published.
- Deakin's own page could not be fetched directly, so the 2027 H452 CSP figure is not confirmed as a Deakin-published number. Policy v27's effective date is also not shown in the extracted text (the schedule says it was first approved on 28 July 2021).

## 2. Which programs exist, which are fully online and part-time, and which require attendance?

CSP (cheap) options: Deakin H452, UNE, UniSQ, Federation and CQU. Full-fee options: Deakin H650, Monash Online, Swinburne Online, ECU Online, UTS Online, UNSW, Adelaide University (via Open Universities Australia), ACAP and Cairnmillar. In Perth, Murdoch's GDPA and UWA honours are on campus. Curtin's fourth year appears to be mainly a stream for its own students. In Melbourne, ACU, RMIT and La Trobe are campus-based. None of the online programs I could read states a compulsory residential or placement.

## 3. Entry requirements and realistic cut-offs

### Takeaway
Published minimums range from 60% (Cairnmillar) to 75% (Deakin H452 external, RMIT). The only hard, published cut-off data I found are **UNE: cut-off WAM 78 for 2026 admission** and **Deakin H650: recent cut-offs of 72% or higher**. Several universities explicitly favour their own students: UWA caps non-UWA graduates at 5% of places, Deakin H452 has a 20-place external quota with a higher minimum, and RMIT and CSU favour internal students. Realistically, an applicant should aim for a **75–80% average in second- and third-year psychology units** to be competitive at the CSP honours programs, and at least 70–72% for the full-fee GDPAs. No program listed an interview for fourth-year entry.

### Cited Findings
- **UNE:** students are "ranked based on their WAM calculated from these units… The cut-off varies from year to year… cut-off WAM for 2026 admission was 78." Applicants must have completed the whole three-year APAC sequence before applying, and a self-assessment eligibility form is required (via search extract) — [UNE Honours Psychology Application Process](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/psychology-fourth-year-program/honours-psychology-application)
- **UNE** rules a/b reference a minimum WAM of 75 (rule a) or 70 (rule b), calculated over specified units such as PSYC200. The extract was noted as possibly outdated — [UNE Psychology Fourth Year Program](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/psychology-fourth-year-program)
- **Deakin:** H452 external 75% plus 65% research methods, with a 20-place external quota. Internal 65%. H650 minimum 65%, recent cut-off 72% or higher (see Q1) — [Deakin policy v27](https://policy.deakin.edu.au/download.php?associated=&id=629&version=27); [Deakin H650](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/graduate-diploma-psychology-advanced)

Completed within the last 10 years (via search extract) — [Monash M5003](https://www.monash.edu/study/courses/find-a-course/psychology-advanced-m5003); [Monash Online](https://online.monash.edu/courses/graduate-diploma-in-psychology-advanced)

## 6. Part-time duration, thesis feasibility with full-time work (38 h/week, 8am–4pm), and placements

### Takeaway
Part-time fourth years normally take **2 years**. Deakin, UNE and UWA put coursework in year 1 and the thesis in year 2. Accelerated online GDPAs take about **16 months to 1.4 years** part-time. The full-time load is officially about **35–40 hours per week** (Deakin, Federation), so part-time is about 17–20 hours per week, on top of a 38-hour job. **No fourth-year program reviewed requires a placement.** The research thesis is the main risk: supervisor meetings, ethics deadlines and participant recruitment. It is feasible part-time, with unpaid leave targeted at proposal, data-collection and write-up peaks.

### Cited Findings
- Deakin H452/H650: full-time load about 35 hours per week; "Part-time: Coursework must be completed in the first year"; duration "may be extended if course requirements are delayed, such as by a placement"; work experience not required — [Deakin H452 Handbook](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H452&stutype=local); [Deakin H650 Handbook](https://handbook.deakin.edu.au/courses-search/course.php?course=H650&stutype=local)
- UNE: "full-time (over one year) or part-time (over two years). Part-time students complete the coursework in the first year and the thesis in the second year." Online honours is usually part-time; full-time needs the Fourth Year Coordinator's permission (via search extract) — [UNE](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/psychology-fourth-year-program/honours-psychology-application)
- UWA: part-time "must be completed in two years"; recommended plan puts thesis units in year 2; supervisor assignments are final — [UWA](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/psychology-honours-application)
- Federation: full-time students "should expect about 35–40 hours per week"; research project is two 30-credit units (PSYCH4001, PSYCH4102) — [Federation](https://www.federation.edu.au/courses/dhy8-bachelor-of-psychological-science-honours/)
- UniSQ: PSY4000 Psychology Honours Project = 4 of 8 units; quota depends on "supervision availability" — [UniSQ](https://www.unisq.edu.au/study/degrees-and-courses/bachelor-of-science-honours-psychology)
- Swinburne Online: Thesis A (Research Project Development, 12.5 cp), Thesis B (Literature Review, 12.5 cp), Thesis C (Empirical Study, 25 cp); "academic supervisors will provide advice and feedback"; 16 months part-time — [Swinburne](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/course/postgraduate/graduate-diploma-of-psychology-advanced-swinburne-online/); [Swinburne PSY80136 Thesis C](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/course/unit/p/psy80136/)

- ACAP: thesis spread across Research Project 1–3, written up as a journal-style manuscript with a conference-style presentation; online students "must devote the same weekly hours per unit" as on-campus (3–4 contact hours per unit per week); part-time up to 6 trimesters — [ACAP](https://www.acap.edu.au/courses/bachelor-of-psychological-science-honours/)
- Cairnmillar: thesis is HON455 (12.5 cp) plus HON456 (25 cp); part-time 18 months or 2 years — [Cairnmillar](https://www.cairnmillar.org.au/course/bachelor-psychology-honours/)
- UNSW: 24 UOC research project split into six courses; admission depends on "topic preferences" and "supervisor availability"; each course runs six weeks — [UNSW](https://www.unsw.edu.au/study/postgraduate/graduate-diploma-in-advanced-psychology)
- Monash Online: part-time, 8 units, about 1.4 years (via search extract) — [Monash Online](https://online.monash.edu/online-courses/psychology-courses/online-graduate-diploma-psychology-advanced/)

### Inferences
- With an 8am–4pm job, evening and weekend study of about 17–20 hours per week matches a part-time load. Supervision meetings and any live online classes may fall in business hours, so negotiate flexibility or a fixed weekly early finish in advance.
- Put unpaid leave blocks at: (1) the start of the thesis (proposal and ethics submission), (2) the data-collection window, and (3) the final 4–6 weeks before thesis submission.
- Programs that put the thesis in year 2 of part-time (UNE, UWA, Deakin) separate coursework and research peaks. Accelerated GDPAs (ECU, UTS, Monash Online) compress the thesis into short teaching periods, for example Swinburne's 25 cp Thesis C in one period, which concentrates the workload.
- The lack of placements in fourth year makes all of these programs compatible with continued full-time employment, unlike fifth-year master's programs.

### Gaps
- No provider publishes how many supervision meetings occur in business hours, or whether online students can run lab-based studies. No official data on completion rates of part-time students who work full time. Forum anecdotes were unavailable.

## 7. Honours vs Graduate Diploma (Advanced): does it matter for master's (fifth-year) admission?

### Takeaway
For **registration and master's eligibility, both are APAC Level 2 fourth years and are treated as equivalent**. Master's selection ranks on **fourth-year marks**, typically requiring an H2A (75%) or equivalent. The practical difference is that a **bachelor honours degree (AQF 8) is the usual PhD entry qualification**, while at least one GDPA states it does *not* qualify graduates for PhD entry. Honours also produces a familiar class (H1/H2A), which avoids grade-conversion questions.

### Cited Findings
- UWA Master of Professional Psychology: requires an accredited psychology honours degree with at least H2A "or an equivalent recognised by the Psychology Board of Australia or APAC". Selection is "based on marks in accredited 4th year psychology course", plus personal statement, referees, CV and possibly an interview — [UWA Handbook 2026 rules 73590](https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/rules?code=73590); [UWA MPP](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/courses/master-of-professional-psychology-coursework)
- Deakin master's (Professional, Clinical, Organisational) entry: "Four year tertiary program with a psychology major from an APAC accredited program completed in the last 10 years with a minimum of a Honours 2A average or equivalent, AND eligibility for registration as a provisional psychologist" — [Deakin policy v27](https://policy.deakin.edu.au/download.php?associated=&id=629&version=27)
- Flinders' one-year APAC fourth-year course "does not qualify graduates for entry into a PhD in psychology" — [Flinders](https://www.flinders.edu.au/study/courses/postgraduate-psychology-advanced)
- GDPA providers describe their programs as honours-equivalent: UNSW "one-year honours-equivalent"; UTS "equivalent to a fourth-year or honours-level psychology qualification"; ECU "equivalent to a fourth-year honours degree" — [UNSW](https://www.unsw.edu.au/study/postgraduate/graduate-diploma-in-advanced-psychology); [UTS Online](https://studyonline.uts.edu.au/online-courses/graduate-diploma-psychology-advanced); [ECU blog](https://studyonline.ecu.edu.au/blog/types-psychology-qualifications)
- TISC grading guidance maps 75–79% to H2A for Curtin, ECU, Murdoch and UWA — [TISC Honours guide](https://www.tisc.edu.au/static/guide/university-admissions-honours-fourth-yr.tisc)

### Inferences
- The fourth-year *mark* matters far more than the award title. A GDPA graduate with 80% is better placed than an honours graduate with H2B. Because master's selection keys on fourth-year marks, choosing a program where the applicant can perform best (a manageable workload alongside work) may matter more than prestige or award type.
- If a PhD or combined Master/PhD (a common route to clinical endorsement) is a possibility, prefer an honours award (Deakin H452, UNE, UniSQ, Federation, ACAP, Cairnmillar, CQU) over a GDPA.

### Gaps
- No master's program found that explicitly excludes GDPA graduates or weights honours above a GDPA. Each target master's (Curtin, UWA, Murdoch, ECU, Monash, Deakin, Cairnmillar) should be checked for how it converts GDPA grades.

## 8. APAC accreditation changes for 2027–2028 and level terminology

### Takeaway
APAC's **2025 Accreditation Standards took effect on 1 December 2025, replacing the 2019 Standards**. The change was mainly an alignment with the Psychology Board's new Code of Conduct and Professional Competencies, which also commenced on 1 December 2025. Current 2027 course pages still use "Level 1 / Level 2" terminology, and I found nothing indicating fourth-year programs lose accreditation in 2027–28. The larger uncertainty is the **Psychology Board's "Higher Degree Redesign"** (consultation April–June 2026, report September 2026, final report due November 2026). It could change the pathway *after* fourth year, but it has no implementation date and depends on government.

### Cited Findings
- "The 2025 Standards took effect on 1 December 2025 and replaced the 2019 Standards". The review aligned APAC standards with PsyBA's Code of conduct and Professional competencies — [APAC – Accreditation Standards Review 2025](https://apac.au/news/accreditation-standards-review-2025/)
- The PsyBA approved the standards on 28 Nov 2025 with effect from 1 Dec 2025. APAC released an updated Evidence Guide on 30 Sep 2026 — [Search results incl. APAC final report Jan 2026](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/APAC-Alignment-Accreditation-Standards-Review-Final-Report-1.pdf); [APAC news list](https://apac.au/news/)
- 2027 course pages still use Level terminology:
  - UniSQ "APAC-accredited Level 2 (fourth-year)" — [UniSQ](https://www.unisq.edu.au/study/degrees-and-courses/bachelor-of-science-honours-psychology)
  - UNSW "Level 2 pre-professional program" — [UNSW](https://www.unsw.edu.au/study/postgraduate/graduate-diploma-in-advanced-psychology)
  - Murdoch "Level 2 course" — [Murdoch](https://www.murdoch.edu.au/course/postgraduate/g1096)
  - Deakin "Foundation Level 1" for three-year sequences — [Deakin policy v27](https://policy.deakin.edu.au/download.php?associated=&id=629&version=27)
- PsyBA Higher Degree Redesign: aims for "a single, shorter and more practical course of study to qualify as a registered psychologist". Public consultation ran 15 Apr–10 Jun 2026. Final report to the Department of Health, Disability and Ageing due November 2026. The project "does not involve implementing those recommendations" (page reviewed 12 Oct 2026) — [PsyBA – Redesigning the higher education pathway](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/About/Education/Redesigning-the-higher-education-pathway.aspx)
- Ahpra (29 Sep 2026): consultation report published; the Board "will continue working to finalise its recommendations for government consideration"; challenges identified include "implementation, supervision capacity, training quality and education provider impacts" — [Ahpra news 29 Sep 2026](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/News/2026-09-29-Psychology-workforce-reform)

- Unverified (search-engine summary only, not confirmed on an official page): "PsyBA says it will recommend that Government progress a five-year accredited program, or equivalent, to general registration" — reported in search results citing [APS consultation document](https://psychology.org.au/getmedia/d73c25ba-90ba-402a-a69f-5bf8aee7abed/psyba-redesign-psych-hd-path-2nd-public-consult-20260601.pdf)

### Inferences
- A fourth year done in 2028–2030 under current APAC Level 2 accreditation should still count toward provisional registration. Any redesign would need government approval and transition arrangements, and none are yet published. The applicant should still monitor the November 2026 final report, because it could change what comes *after* fourth year (5+1 vs master's) by the time they finish (~2029–30).
- I found no evidence that the 2025 Standards renamed the Levels or changed fourth-year entry rules for 2027–28.

### Gaps
- The full text of the 2025 Standards (structure and transition provisions) and the PsyBA consultation report were not read. Transition or grandfathering arrangements for students starting fourth year in 2028 are not published.
- The "five-year program" recommendation could not be verified on a PsyBA page. Treat it as unconfirmed (the registration-rules researcher should cover this).
