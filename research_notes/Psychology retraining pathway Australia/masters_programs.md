# Accredited fifth-year and fifth-plus-sixth-year professional psychology programs: Perth, Melbourne and online (as at 25 Nov 2026)

Notes compiled 25 November 2026. All dollar figures are AUD. Confidence tags used throughout:
- **[V]**: official university or regulator page fetched directly on 25 Nov 2026.
- **[S]**: official-domain page whose text came only through a search-engine summary, because the page blocked direct fetching (HTTP 403/503). Moderate confidence. Re-check before relying on it.
- **[A]**: third-party aggregator (Mastersportal, Hotcourses, Good Universities Guide, TopUniversities, globalhealtheducation, etc.). Low confidence.

Terminology: "Level 3" means an APAC fifth-year (MPP-type) program leading to the 5+1 internship. "Level 4" means a sixth-year specialist (clinical, etc.) program; a packaged Level 3+4 two-year master's leads straight to general registration. Many official pages blocked automated fetching (UNE, CQU, Monash, Deakin, ECU, La Trobe, ACU, University of Melbourne). Their figures are marked [S] or [A].

**Timing note for this applicant:** every 2027 intake below has already closed, or closes by 30 Nov 2026. Someone finishing a fourth year in 2028–29 would apply in the Aug–Nov 2028 or 2029 round, for 2029 or 2030 entry. The 2027 fees below stand in for those years and will be indexed upward.

## Q1. Which accredited programs exist in Perth, Melbourne and online, and what are their key parameters?

### Takeaway
**Perth:**
- Curtin runs a two-step model: a 1-year Master of Psychology (Professional), then a 1-year Master of Clinical Psychology. Curtin is also launching a new 2-year Master of Psychology (Clinical), which is still seeking APAC accreditation.
- UWA has a 1-year MPP and a 2-year Master of Clinical Psychology.
- Murdoch's two programs are 2-year, on campus, and about $72k at full fee. One of them is a packaged honours-plus-MPP that admits people straight from a 3-year degree.
- ECU has an MPP and a Master of Clinical Psychology, but its pages could not be fetched.

**Melbourne:**
- Two-year clinical master's: Swinburne, RMIT, University of Melbourne, Deakin, Monash and La Trobe.
- MPPs: Monash, Deakin, VU, ACU, Federation and Cairnmillar.
- University of Melbourne also has a 2-year MPP that admits people from a 3-year degree.

**Online or largely online:** UNE MPP (online with intensives; described as the only fully online fifth year), Cairnmillar MPP (online or mixed), CQU MPP (online plus 3 residential schools), SCU MPP (online), La Trobe MPP (online), UniSQ MPP (external with residentials). All of them still need in-person or supervised placement hours.

### Inferences
- **Two ways to skip a separate honours year exist in the target cities:** Murdoch M1388 (packaged Level 2+3, 2 yrs, about $72k full fee) and University of Melbourne MC-PROPSYC (2 yrs, CSP available). Both are on campus. Both are relevant only if the applicant has not already committed to a separate fourth year.
- **Curtin's two-step model:** a clinical endorsement route in Perth can be MPP (CSP $18,100) plus MC-CLIPS (CSP $5,000), about $23,100 CSP across 2 years at 2027 rates. Its 2-year MC-PSYCLI was not yet accredited as at 25 Nov 2026.
- **Most worker-compatible delivery:** UNE (online, part-time up to 4 years, 3 trimester intakes) and Cairnmillar (online, part-time over 2 years). Both still need a concentrated placement block. Neither is CSP. Federation's "flexible learning" mode at Berwick is CSP but involves interviews and placements in Victoria.

### Gaps
- ECU (MPP and MClinPsych): fees, CSP status, entry thresholds and placement details are unverified because the pages blocked fetching.
- UWA MPP and MClinPsych: no dollar figures published on the course pages, and CSP is not explicitly confirmed.
- Murdoch: whether M1388/M1156 offer any CSPs is not stated. $36k/yr looks like a full-fee figure.
- Monash D6008/M6046, Deakin MPP/MPsych Clinical, La Trobe MPP/MClinPsych, ACU MPP campus, University of Melbourne amounts: not verified from official pages.
- Not researched: Federation DYL9 Master of Psychology (Clinical), Cairnmillar Master of Psychology (Clinical), Charles Sturt University MPP (mode unknown), Monash "Master of Psychology" variants, ACAP clinical offerings.
- APAC's own accredited-program list (apac.au) was not retrieved. Accreditation statements come from provider pages only.

## Q2. Which programs are CSP in 2027/2028, what does a CSP place cost, and which are full fee and how much? (Includes checking the applicant's earlier figures)

### Takeaway
- **The fee band decides the CSP price.** Clinical master's units are charged at the lowest band, **$4,908 per full-time year in 2027** (about $9,800 for a 2-year program). Many MPPs fall in the behavioural-science band, about **$17,400–$18,100 per year**.
- **The applicant's earlier figures look internally consistent with 2027 rates.** The gap between the two totals ($8,200) matches Curtin MPP CSP ($18,100) minus a 2-year clinical CSP ($9,816).
- **Pathway B depends on a scarce place.** Curtin's MPP CSPs are "limited". Without one, the MPP costs $42,042 full fee.
- Most private and online MPPs are full fee only, at about $35k–$38k.

## Q3. How competitive is entry (fourth-year grade needed, interviews, places vs applicants)?

### Takeaway
- Published floors are **H2A / about 70%** for most MPPs and some clinical programs, and **80%** at RMIT and Monash clinical. All programs rank applicants and interview a shortlist, and minimums do not guarantee a place.
- System-wide, only about **10% of postgraduate psychology applicants** found a place, according to APS (Sept 2024).
- Exact applicant-to-place ratios are not published by any provider found.

### Cited Findings
- "Nine in ten aspiring psychologists are excluded from the profession by limited university offerings". The sector has space for "only 10 per cent of all postgraduate applicants". "79 of 187 national psychology courses have stopped intakes". — [V] [APS Insights, 23 Sep 2024 (republished from The Australian)](https://psychology.org.au/insights/psychology-student-grants-first-step-in-‘future-pr)
- **Published grade floors:**

## Q4. What do placements involve, and can someone working full-time on weekdays complete them?

### Takeaway
- MPPs require about **300–315 placement hours**. That is roughly 8 weeks full-time, or one trimester at 3 days a week. Two-year clinical master's require about **1,000 hours or 130+ days**, roughly 6+ months full-time equivalent, during business hours.
- No program found is explicitly designed for full-time workers, and none offers evening or weekend placements. UWA notes only that "early evening appointments" are "sometimes needed".
- The most worker-compatible option is a **part-time online MPP** (UNE up to 4 years; Cairnmillar 2 years), with **one block of unpaid leave** for the placement.

### Cited Findings
- **UNE MPP placement:**
  - 300 hours in the final trimester, "equates to 24 hours or 3 days per week".
  - "Placement cannot be condensed into a shorter timeframe, nor can it be extended beyond the end of the trimester".
  - Described as a simulated, online placement (PSYC533).
  — [S] [UNE MPP FAQ](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/clinical-psychology-postgraduate-courses/MPP-frequently-asked-questions); [S] [UNE 2027](https://www.une.edu.au/study/courses/2027/master-of-professional-psychology)
- **UWA MPP placement:** "8 weeks of full-time placement, or a part-time equivalent" in semester 2. Inherent requirements cite "full-day placements" and "early evening appointments" that are "sometimes needed". The course is full-time only. — [S] [UWA handbook](https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/coursedetails?code=73590); [V] [UWA MPP](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/courses/master-of-professional-psychology-coursework)
- **Other MPP placements:**

  - UNE: mandatory intensive schools (MPsych FAQ: about Easter and August). — [S] [UNE](https://www.une.edu.au/about-une/faculty-of-medicine-and-health/school-of-psychology/future-students/clinical-psychology-postgraduate-courses/mpsych-faqs)

### Inferences
- **Arithmetic (estimates):**
  - 300 hours ÷ 7.6 h/day ≈ 40 working days ≈ **8 weeks of full-time unpaid leave**, or about 12.5 weeks at 3 days a week (UNE's fixed pattern).
  - 1,000 hours ÷ 38 h/week ≈ **26 weeks full-time equivalent**, in addition to weekday coursework and clinic sessions in 2-year clinical programs.
- **A full-time 8am–4pm worker:**
  - Could plausibly complete a part-time online MPP (UNE, Cairnmillar) by taking **one block of about 8–13 weeks of leave** for the placement.
  - Is very unlikely to complete a 2-year clinical master's without reducing to part-time work or taking about 6+ months of cumulative leave. Internal clinic sessions at RMIT and Swinburne run in business hours.
- **UNE's 300-hour placement is described as simulated and online.** If confirmed, this is the most logistically flexible location-wise. However, it is fixed at 3 days a week for a full trimester and can't be compressed into a leave block.

### Gaps
- No program states that evening or weekend placement scheduling is available. Not confirmed either way for Cairnmillar, La Trobe online or SCU.
- Whether interstate (WA) students in Victorian or online programs can do placements locally is unconfirmed for Cairnmillar, La Trobe and Federation. SCU says local placement is not guaranteed.
- CPP eligibility conditions for psychology (CSP-only? means test? whether full-time employees qualify) were not confirmed. The 2026 rate is $338.60/week per the APS/Department of Education summary.

## Q5. Can the 5+1 internship after an MPP be done part-time while working, and with which employers?

### Takeaway
- **Part-time is permitted:** "one-year full-time program (or equivalent part-time)", with no maximum timeframe. But **every hour must come from a Board-approved psychological work role**, so an unrelated full-time job does not count.
- The internship needs **1,500 hours, including ≥500 client-contact hours**. Client contact can be by **videoconference or telephone**.
- Paid intern roles exist with WA Department of Education school psychology, Victorian public-sector provisional psychologist roles (including part-time), and federally funded internship programs (Cairnmillar's G-PIP: up to 600 places).

### Cited Findings
- **Requirements from the Guidelines for the 5+1 internship program (effective 1 Dec 2025):**
  - **1,500 hours** of psychological practice, with "client contact hours… a minimum of 500 hours". "Up to 60 of the 500 hours… can be simulated learning".
  - **80 hours supervision**, "a minimum of 50 hours… individual". About 1 hour of supervision per 18 hours of practice. At least 4 direct observations.
  - **Minimum 60 hours** of education and training.
  - "The minimum time for completing the 5+1 internship is **44 weeks full time**… While there is **no maximum timeframe** for completion, applicants must meet the requirements of the Recency of Practice standard".
  - Provisional registration "may be renewed twice… but cannot be held for more than three years unless a new application is made".
  - Logbook reviewed "weekly, or fortnightly if part-time".
  — [V] [PsyBA Guidelines for the 5+1 internship program, Dec 2025 (PDF via Ahpra)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d); [V] [PsyBA news, 8 Sep 2025](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/News/2025-09-08-Guidelines-for-internship-program-released.aspx)
- **Client contact "may be in person, by videoconference, or telephone".** — [V] [Guidelines s4.1.1](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)
- **Work roles must be psychological:**
  - "If an applicant intends to undertake employment… unrelated to psychology in another professional field, their proposed 5+1 internship plan will not be accepted". Teaching or tutoring in psychology and research positions "will not be approved".
  - "Ahpra will consider applications for **concurrent work roles** on application".
  - Interns may not run an independent private practice, but "the Board may approve applications to work in a private practice with other psychologists".
  — [V] [Guidelines s4.2](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)
- **Supervision arrangements:**
  - The principal supervisor is expected onsite. Offsite is approved if there is "adequate onsite workplace supervision". — [V] [Guidelines s5.4.6](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)
  - Video supervision is allowed if participants "can clearly see each other's facial expressions and body language". — [V] [PsyBA Internship FAQ (5+1)](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Internship-FAQ-5-1)
- **The intern finds the role:** "You need to arrange your own internship by finding a suitable work role and supervisor". The National Psychology Exam is required before general registration. — [V] [PsyBA FAQ](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Internship-FAQ-5-1); [V] [PsyBA 5+1 page](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/5-1-Internship-Program.aspx)
- **Employers:**
  - WA Department of Education supports provisionally registered psychologists on the 4+2 or 5+1 pathway with Board-approved supervision. Its "Graduate and New Talent School Psychologist Register" (closes 31 Dec 2026) is not a guarantee of employment. School psychologist pay is Level 1–2 **$93,706–$137,373** p.a. (pro-rata). — [S] [WA Education new graduates](https://www.education.wa.edu.au/new-graduates); [S] [WA jobs board](https://search.jobs.wa.gov.au/jobs/graduate-and-new-talent-school-psychologist-register-gascoyne-region-western-australia-australia-goldfields-esperance-region-perth-metropolitan-kimberley-region-great-southern-region-wheatbel)
  - A Victorian Government "Provisional Psychologist" role was advertised as "Fixed-term – Part-time", which shows part-time provisional roles exist in the Victorian public sector. — [S] [careers.vic.gov.au](https://www.careers.vic.gov.au/job/provisional-psychologist-64691)
  - Cairnmillar's Graduate Psychology Internship Program (G-PIP) has a "new three-year federal grant" to "support up to 600 internships nationally" for 5+1 interns. Eligibility: APAC-accredited fifth year plus provisional-registration eligibility. — [S] [Cairnmillar G-PIP](https://www.cairnmillar.org.au/graduate-psychology-internship-program-g-pip-expanded-cairnmillar-secures-new-federal-funding-for-600-psychology-internships/)

### Inferences
- **Arithmetic (estimate):** 1,500 hours part-time at about 15 h/week ≈ 100 weeks (about 2 years); at 19 h/week (0.5 FTE) ≈ 79 weeks.
- **What this means for the applicant:**
  - Keeping a non-psychology full-time job means the internship hours must come on top of it, from a second, approved psychological role.
  - Accumulating 500 client-contact hours only in evenings and weekends is theoretically allowed. Concurrent roles are considered, and telehealth client contact counts. In practice it depends on finding an employer and supervisor offering such hours.
  - The more realistic options are a part-time psychological role (for example, 0.5 FTE in a school, NGO or telehealth service) or a block of leave.
- A provisional psychologist can work within a group private or telehealth practice if the Board approves the plan and supervision is adequate, which fits the long-term telehealth goal. They cannot operate independently.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of telehealth companies specifically advertising 5+1 intern roles, or of their supervision arrangements.
- Pay rates for 5+1 interns outside WA schools were not researched.
- The Recency of Practice standard's interaction with a very long part-time internship was not checked.
