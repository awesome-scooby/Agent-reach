# Online APAC-accredited psychology conversion courses (graduate diplomas, excluding UNE) for a Perth graduate who may move to Melbourne in 2027

Researcher notes, compiled 25 November 2026. All pages were accessed on 25 Nov 2026 unless stated otherwise. All amounts are AUD.

**Research limits (read first).** Deakin (deakin.edu.au), University of Newcastle (newcastle.edu.au), Monash (monash.edu, online.monash.edu), Charles Sturt (study.csu.edu.au), JCU main site and the ACU handbook all returned Cloudflare/403 "Just a moment" bot-challenge pages to every fetch method. I did not bypass those challenges. For those providers, facts come from (a) search-engine snippets of the official pages, (b) Open Universities Australia (OUA) listings, (c) official PDFs that did load, or (d) third-party aggregators. Each finding is labelled. Where a figure came only from a search snippet, it is marked "(snippet)" and should be re-checked on the live page before any money is committed.

**Applicant profile used for the assessment:** Australian citizen in Perth. Pakistani BEng (2016) plus La Trobe MEngMgmt (2020, AQF 9). Works 38 h/week, 8am–4pm. Goal is general registration as a psychologist.

## 1. What credible online conversion courses exist for 2027, and how do they compare? (master comparison, plus checks on the applicant's three figures)

### Takeaway
I found at least 14 live, APAC-accredited (or accredited-with-conditions) online conversion courses for 2027, not counting UNE. Every confirmed fully online option is **full-fee (FEE-HELP)**. The applicant's three figures check out as follows:
- **Newcastle $36,850:** verified, and it is the full-fee price.
- **ECU Online $31,280 / 16 months:** verified.
- **Deakin $41,000:** could not be verified. The official page is blocked; third-party figures range from $32,800 to $41,400.

For someone working 38 h/week, the best-fitting formats are the 7-week "one unit at a time" models: ECU Online, UNSW, UTS, JCU and Monash Online. RMIT Online (10-week terms, 10–12 h/week) also fits well.

## 2. Which offer Commonwealth Supported Places (CSPs) in 2027, what is the actual student contribution, and which are full-fee only?

### Takeaway
I found **no fully online conversion course (outside UNE) that clearly advertises CSPs for 2027**. All are full-fee with FEE-HELP.
- **UoN** is the only plausible CSP option. It listed a CSP rate for this diploma in 2025 and says CSPs exist across its postgraduate programs, but places are limited, and the 2025 CSP figure came from a page about Indigenous students' CSP eligibility.
- **Rough cost if a CSP were offered:** a 2027 CSP for a 1-EFTSL conversion diploma would cost about **$18,025** if units are charged at the Band 4 "behavioural science" rate. It would cost **$9,880** if the lower "professional pathway psychology" rate applied. The 2025 UoN and CSU figures suggest the Band 4 rate is what conversion diplomas actually charge.

### Inferences
- **Arithmetic for a hypothetical 2027 CSP.** An 8-unit or 10-unit graduate diploma is 1.0 EFTSL.
  - At the Band 4 rate: 1.0 × $18,025 = **$18,025 total**. That is $2,253 per unit for an 8-unit course (0.125 EFTSL each) or $1,802.50 per unit for a 10-unit course (0.1 EFTSL each).
  - At the professional pathway rate: 1.0 × $9,880 = **$9,880**.
  - Statistics units coded as mathematics (Band 1, $4,908/EFTSL) would lower the total slightly.
- **Why Band 4 is the likely rate.** UoN's 2025 CSP figure of $16,992, and CSU's identical figure, sit just below the 2026 Band 4 rate of $17,399 (16,992 × 1.024 ≈ 17,400). That points to conversion diplomas being charged the Band 4 behavioural-science rate, not the professional-pathway rate. This is an inference; I did not find a regulatory definition confirming it.
- **Savings versus full fee.** Compared with UoN's $36,850 full fee, a UoN CSP in 2027 would save about $18,800 at the Band 4 rate. Against ECU Online ($31,280), the saving would be about $13,250.
- **Practical conclusion.** For an online course the applicant can start in 2027, budget for full fee ($24.7k–$45k) using FEE-HELP. Ask UoN directly whether a domestic CSP is offered for the GDipPsychSc in 2027, and how CSPs are allocated.

### Gaps
- UoN's 2027 CSP list and its 2027 student-contribution PDF were blocked to me, so I could not confirm whether non-Indigenous domestic students can get a CSP in the GDipPsychSc in 2027.
- I did not find an authoritative Department of Education definition of "professional pathway psychology". Whether conversion-diploma units can attract the $9,880 rate is therefore unconfirmed. The department's indexed-rates PDF returned HTTP 503.
- The 2026/2027 FEE-HELP lifetime limit was not retrieved. The applicant should check how much of the limit their La Trobe master's used, if it was funded with FEE-HELP.
