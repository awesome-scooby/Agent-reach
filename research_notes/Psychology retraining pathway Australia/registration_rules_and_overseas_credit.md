"Verified" means I read the primary document myself. "Search extract" means the figure came from a search-engine summary and I did not open the source page.

## Q0. Applicant's current understanding: verified or corrected

### Takeaway
The applicant's model is mostly right, with five corrections:
1. Provisional registration requires *enrolment* in the 5th-year or higher-degree program. An offer or acceptance alone is not enough in practice.
2. The fourth year must have been completed within the last 10 years.
3. 4+2 closed to new entrants on 30 June 2022 and is retired on 1 January 2029.
4. Higher-degree graduates do not sit the NPE. Only 5+1 interns, legacy 4+2 interns and overseas-trained applicants do.
5. A 2026 Board proposal would abolish the 5+1 pathway, provisional registration and the NPE in favour of a single 5-year degree. As of November 2026 this is not approved or in force.

### Cited Findings
- **Fourth year, then provisional registration (verified).** The minimum qualification for provisional registration is "a four-year accredited sequence of study in psychology completed in the last 10 years, or a sequence of study that, in the Board's opinion, is substantially equivalent to an approved qualification." — [PsyBA Registration standard: Provisional registration, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)
- **Accredited fourth year (verified).** The Board describes the usual 5+1 sequence as "a three-year Bachelor Degree (or equivalent bridging sequence) followed by a one-year Bachelor Honours Degree (or equivalent) then a one-year Masters Degree (e.g. Masters of Professional Psychology)". Students apply for provisional registration before starting the Masters, using their fourth-year qualification. — [PsyBA Policy for provisional registration in combined 4th and 5th year programs, 8 Oct 2020 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD20%2f30289&dbid=AP&chksum=aq2ZjYjsMu1I%2bO28g64lGg%3d%3d)
- **Both routes to general registration (verified).** Under the General registration standard (effective 1 Dec 2025), the supervised practice program must be one of these:
  - (i) a Board-approved postgraduate qualification accredited as fifth and sixth year
  - (ii) all 5th/6th-year coursework and practicum of a 5th–7th-year doctorate, plus sufficient thesis progress
  - (iii) an accredited fifth year followed by a Board-approved 5+1 internship
  - (iv) a Board-approved 4+2 internship (legacy only)
  - (v) an overseas qualification plus a transitional program

  The standard also requires "a six-year sequence of education and training" and passing the NPE "(unless exempt)". — [PsyBA Registration standard: General registration, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33836&dbid=AP&chksum=%2fewcsB%2bopxW0wQs78bCldw%3d%3d)
- **Who sits the NPE (verified).** The groups who must pass it are 4+2 interns, 5+1 interns and internationally trained psychologists (unless exempt). Higher-degree graduates are not on the list. The Board explains that the internships are "non-accredited pathways". — [PsyBA Guidelines for the National Psychology Exam, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f34997&dbid=AP&chksum=cTg4k9XkSVP7Kn%2b3tW%2frCw%3d%3d)
- **4+2 closure (verified).** The 4+2 "closed to new applications on 30 June 2022". — [PsyBA 4+2 internship program page, reviewed 6 Mar 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/4-2-Internship-Program.aspx)
- **No mixing of pathways (verified).** "A provisional psychologist is not allowed to carry out dual pathways; that is, mixing components of two different pathways to make up a six-year sequence of study." — [Provisional registration standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)
- **Program names (verified).** The two-year higher degree is a "Masters degree (coursework)" accredited at Level 3 and 4, e.g. Master of Psychology (Clinical), Master of Clinical Psychology or Master of Professional Psychology & Counselling. The one-year fifth year is typically the Master of Professional Psychology (Level 3). — [APAC Evidence Guide v1.4, Dec 2024 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Evidence-Guide-v1.4-final.pdf); [PsyBA 5+1 internship page, reviewed 13 Jul 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/5-1-Internship-Program.aspx)

### Inferences
- The "Master of Professional Psychology" is the *one-year* fifth year (5+1 route), not a two-year degree. The two-year route uses Master of Psychology / Clinical Psychology or other area-of-practice masters, or the combined MPsych/PhD or DPsych.
- With an engineering bachelor's, the applicant must first complete an accredited Level 1 sequence (e.g. a Graduate Diploma of Psychology) and then a Level 2 fourth year (e.g. a Graduate Diploma of Psychology (Advanced) or Honours) before any of the above applies. Course options are covered by another researcher.

### Gaps
- None for this summary. Details are in the sections below.

## Q1. Current PsyBA registration standards: provisional eligibility wording, meaning of "enrolled" or "accepted", duration limits, fees and processing times

### Takeaway
Provisional registration (standard effective 1 December 2025) requires:
- an accredited 4-year sequence completed in the last 10 years, and
- *needing* provisional registration to complete an approved 5th/6th-year degree or a 5+1 program.

In practice Ahpra wants fourth-year results plus *proof of fifth-year enrolment* from the university. Registration is granted for 12 months and renewed annually. It can be renewed twice (maximum 3 years per application), after which a new application is needed.

Fees on the schedule effective 1 October 2026:
- provisional: $0 application fee and $493 annual registration fee
- general: $604 application fee plus $493 registration fee
- NPE: $450

Ahpra aims to finalise complete graduate applications within about 2 weeks of receiving results.

### Cited Findings
**Eligibility wording (verified).** The applicant must meet suitability and other standards, and "(d) the individual needs provisional registration in order to complete one of the following programs of supervised practice:
- enrolment in a Board-approved qualification accredited as a fifth and sixth, or fifth to seventh year of study, or
- a Board-approved 5+1 internship program – comprising a Board-approved qualification accredited as a fifth year of study, followed by a Board-approved internship …, or
- a Board-approved 4+2 internship program …, or
- a Board-approved transitional program as specified by the Board."

Source: [Provisional registration standard, effective 1 Dec 2025, approved by Ministerial Council 8 Jul 2024 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)

**Maintaining registration (verified).**
- "Provisional registration must be maintained for the entire duration of the supervised practice program … including while awaiting the outcome of an application for general registration."
- Higher-degree candidates must hold it "from the start of enrolment and for the duration of enrolment in any component of an accredited qualification. This includes coursework, practical and research thesis."
- It "must be renewed annually." The same standard says it replaces the version dated 1 June 2017.

Source: [Provisional registration standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)

**What "accepted" or "enrolled" means in practice:**
- The Board's 2025 graduate notice says you can apply "when you have completed your fourth year of psychology study" and "have been accepted into a fifth-year degree or higher degree pathway". Your education provider must send "your graduate results and enrolment listing" to Ahpra. — [PsyBA news, 18 Sep 2025](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/News/2025-09-18-graduate-registration-opens.aspx)
- The current provisional page says: "Your education provider must send your fourth-year results and proof of fifth-year enrolment to Ahpra." Recency of practice is automatically met if the approved program was completed within the last five years. — [PsyBA Provisional registration page, reviewed 13 Jul 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional.aspx)
- HISTORICAL (FAQ reviewed 19 Jul 2019): "AHPRA will not accept a letter of offer to enrol in a course." A print-out of the online enrolment record is acceptable, and it is cross-checked against a list from the head of psychology. Higher-degree students must apply after enrolling and before starting. They "must not start any supervised practice placement or have any client contact" until approved. — [PsyBA Higher degree FAQ](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Higher-Degree-FAQ.aspx)
- Combined 4th+5th-year programs: provisional registration is considered once the student has completed all fourth-year requirements, is enrolled and continuing into the fifth-year component, and would be awarded an approved fourth-year qualification if they exit. Evidence must come directly from the provider. — [Combined 4th/5th-year policy, 8 Oct 2020 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD20%2f30289&dbid=AP&chksum=aq2ZjYjsMu1I%2bO28g64lGg%3d%3d)

**Duration limits (verified).**
- "Provisional registration is granted for one year from the date your application is approved … cannot be renewed more than twice." If not finished within 3 years, a new application is needed. Lodge at least two months before expiry. — [PsyBA Provisional registration page, reviewed 13 Jul 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional.aspx)
- The 5+1 guidelines say registration "may be renewed twice if required (see section 64(3) of the National Law) but cannot be held for more than three years unless a new application is made". A new application is due at least 60 days before the end of year 3. Provisional registration is renewed every 12 months from approval, whereas general registration renews by 30 November. — [Guidelines for the 5+1 internship program, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)
- The provisional standard itself sets no overall cap. The only explicit maximum is in the 4+2 definition: "minimum of two years and a maximum of five years". — [Provisional registration standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)

### Inferences
- The 2026/27 national figures (provisional total $493 in year one; general $604 + $493 = $1,097 in the year of transition) come from the schedule labelled "effective 1 October 2026". These almost certainly apply to the registration year 1 Dec 2026 – 30 Nov 2027, but the page does not state the period.
- Plan on provisional registration costing about $493 per year. A part-time 5th year plus a part-time internship would need about 3 renewals and possibly a fresh application after year 3.
- The 10-year recency rule on the fourth year matters for a slow part-time learner. If the fourth year is finished more than 10 years before applying for provisional or general registration, it no longer qualifies.

## Q2. 4+2 closure, transition, and any new pathway or reform in 2025–2026 (enacted vs proposed)

### Takeaway
**Enacted:**
- 4+2 closed to new entrants on 30 June 2022. Current interns had to apply for general registration by 30 June 2027, and the pathway is retired on 1 January 2029.
- Revised General and Provisional standards, new 5+1 guidelines, new NPE guidelines and new professional competencies all took effect on 1 December 2025.
- Commonwealth-funded 5+1 supervision scholarships are running for 2026–2030.

**Proposed only (not approved):**
- The Board's "Redesigning the higher education pathway" project recommends a single 5-year accredited degree (proposed at AQF 8) leading directly to general registration. It would have no internship, no NPE and no provisional registration.
- Area of practice endorsement would require a separate 2-year postgraduate program.
- Government support is required, transition arrangements are undefined, and the Board says current requirements remain in place.

### Cited Findings
**4+2 closure and retirement (verified).**
- 4+2 interns "have a maximum of five years to complete" (up to 12 months' extension possible). They must complete all requirements, including the NPE, and apply for general registration by 30 June 2027. Applications via 4+2 "cannot be accepted after 1 January 2029", when the pathway is retired and the new standards take effect. — [PsyBA 4+2 page, reviewed 6 Mar 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/4-2-Internship-Program.aspx)
- HISTORICAL: these dates were announced on 24 May 2019 (Ministerial Council approved 3 Apr 2019). — [PsyBA news, 24 May 2019](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/News/2019-05-24-retirement-of-the-4-plus-2-internship-pathway.aspx); [Retirement of 4+2 page, reviewed 1 Jul 2022](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/Retirement-of-4-2-internship.aspx)
- The Registration Standards page separately notes: "The Ministerial Council approved changes to the Provisional and General Registration Standards, effective 1 January 2029". This is the 4+2 retirement, not the 2026 redesign. — [PsyBA Registration standards page](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/Registration-Standards.aspx)

**Enacted 1 December 2025 (verified).**
- General and Provisional registration standards took effect 1 Dec 2025 (approved by Ministerial Council 8 Jul 2024; next review January 2029). — [General standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33836&dbid=AP&chksum=%2fewcsB%2bopxW0wQs78bCldw%3d%3d)
- The updated 5+1 guidelines took effect 1 Dec 2025 "alongside the updated professional competencies and new Code of conduct". The case-study requirement was removed and lifted immediately for all 5+1 interns. — [PsyBA news, 8 Sep 2025](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/News/2025-09-08-Guidelines-for-internship-program-released.aspx)
- Other current documents: the NPE guidelines (1 Dec 2025) and the Criminal History standard (15 Jul 2026). Fourteen boards consulted on revised recency of practice and CPD standards until 17 Jul 2026. — [PsyBA Codes, guidelines and policies](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/Codes-Guidelines-Policies.aspx); [Registration standards page](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/Registration-Standards.aspx)

**Proposed: the higher-degree redesign project.**
- **Background (verified).** The Department of Health, Disability and Ageing asked the Board to "recommend changes to the six-year psychology training pathway".
  - Public consultation ran 15 Apr – 10 Jun 2026, with forums in 8 cities including Perth and Melbourne.
  - The final report to the Department was scheduled for November 2026.
  - The project "does not involve implementing those recommendations".
  - Source: [PsyBA Redesigning the higher education pathway, reviewed 12 Oct 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/About/Education/Redesigning-the-higher-education-pathway.aspx)
- **Consultation report (verified).** The report (2,500 submissions) says the Board "will recommend to Government that the general registration standard is updated to reflect that the qualification is a five-year accredited program". The AoPE standard would change "to reflect that the qualification is a two-year accredited postgraduate program". The psychology assistant role would be removed from the current reform.
  - The consulted model is a "Bachelor of Professional Psychology as a single, direct entry, undergraduate qualification (AQF Level 8)".
  - The Board will engage stakeholders on implementation "if the proposed model is supported by Government".
  - Transition arrangements are listed as an implementation issue but are not designed.
  - The report says providers keep flexibility to create graduate-entry programs, bridging programs or RPL.
  - Source: [PsyBA public consultation report, Sept 2026 (PDF, hosted by AAPi)](https://www.aapi.org.au/common/Uploaded%20files/AAPi/Redesigning-the-psychology-higher-degree-program-Sept-2026.PDF)
- **Ahpra summary (verified, 29 Sep 2026).** Feedback "will inform the Board's advice and recommendations to government". There is no timeline. Concerns raised were supervision capacity, training quality and provider impacts. — [Ahpra news, 29 Sep 2026](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/News/2026-09-29-Psychology-workforce-reform)
- **Professional association summary (verified, 29 Sep 2026).** The model has "no internship, provisional registration or NPE". The reforms are "not yet approved or in effect", "current education and registration requirements remain in place", and "Any changes will require Government support before they can proceed". — [AAPi news](https://www.aapi.org.au/Web/Web/About-AAPi/News/Articles/2026/Sept/Psychology_Board_Education_and_Training_Reform_Update.aspx)
- **Health Ministers' timing (search extract, unverified secondary).** Consideration by Health Ministers has reportedly been deferred from December 2026 to 2027. — [PsychVault blog](https://psychvault.com.au/blog/proposed-five-year-psychology-pathway)

**Commonwealth funding (enacted program; figures partly search extracts).**
- The "Addressing Critical Psychology Shortages – Supporting Provisional Psychologists to Practice" extension provides "$47.6 million over three financial years from 2026–27", expected to support "approximately 500 internships each year through to 2028–29".
- Provisional Psychologist Hub runs a part-time scholarship (SPPP):
  - Eligibility: 5+1 interns, citizens or permanent residents, doing 17–29 hours of psychological practice per week.
  - Covers up to 95 hours of individual and 13 hours of group supervision, for up to 18 months.
- CatholicCare Victoria Tasmania runs a scholarship for 30+ hours/week: up to 84 individual and 12 group supervision hours, with "350 supervision scholarships through to 2030".

- Cairnmillar's Graduate Psychology Internship Program says it supports "up to 600 internships nationally" and is "free until June 2027" for domestic provisional psychologists (search extract). — [Cairnmillar G-PIP](https://www.cairnmillar.org.au/study-at-cmi/find-a-course/pd/gpip/)

### Inferences
- For planning in 2027–2030, the applicant should assume the current rules (5+1 or two-year higher degree) apply, while watching for transition rules.
- Any new 5-year single degree would need government endorsement, accreditation standards and new programs. That makes it unlikely to affect someone entering a fourth year or a 5th year before about 2028–2029. This is my estimate, not a source statement.
- The reform report mentions graduate-entry and bridging flexibility. That may matter for career-changers but is undefined.
- Funded supervision scholarships materially reduce the cost of a 5+1 internship, and the SPPP specifically supports part-time internships (17–29 h/week).

### Gaps
- No official government decision or ministerial communiqué on the redesign was found as of 25 Nov 2026.
- The Board's "Higher education FAQs, updated October 2026" exist but I did not read them. They may address transition for current students.
- The GrantConnect figures (number of internships, 1,500 commitment) come from search extracts. The official grant guidelines were not opened.
- No separate "4+1+1" pathway was found in any PsyBA document.

## Q3. 5+1 internship: hours, supervision, client contact, cost, part-time, telehealth, and the NPE

### Takeaway
The internship totals 1,500 hours of psychological practice:
- at least 500 hours of client contact (up to 60 of which can be simulated)
- 80 hours of supervision (at least 50 individual with the principal supervisor)
- at least 60 hours of education and training

The minimum is 44 weeks full-time. Part-time is allowed, there is no maximum timeframe (subject to recency), and up to 8 weeks of leave a year is allowed without notifying the Board.

Client contact and supervision may be in person, by video or by phone. The work role must be psychological in nature (paid or unpaid). Independent private practice is not allowed.

The NPE costs $450 and has four sittings a year (Feb, May, Aug, Nov), at a test centre or by online proctoring, with a 70% scaled pass mark. After 3 failures there is a waiting/plan process; after 6, the Board may refuse renewal.

### Cited Findings
**Table 1 requirements (verified).** All from the [Guidelines for the 5+1 internship program, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d):
- Psychological practice: 1,500 hours (including supervision and education/training).
- Client contact: "a minimum of 500 hours", of which "Up to 60 … can be simulated learning".
- Supervision: 80 hours, "approximately 1 hour of supervision for every 18 hours of practice". At least 50 hours must be individual supervision with the principal supervisor. Up to 30 hours may be group supervision or supervision with a secondary supervisor.
- Direct observation: at least 4 sessions every 6 months (2 assessment, 2 intervention).
- Education and training: at least 60 hours.

**Duration (verified, same source).**
- "The minimum time for completing the 5+1 internship is 44 weeks full time … While there is no maximum timeframe for completion, applicants must meet the requirements of the Recency of Practice standard."
- Ahpra "will not consider an application for general registration until the minimum internship period of 44 weeks has been completed."
- The Board describes the internship as "one-year full-time program (or equivalent part-time)". — [PsyBA 5+1 page, reviewed 13 Jul 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Provisional/5-1-Internship-Program.aspx)

**Leave (verified).** All from the [5+1 guidelines (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d):
- "Up to eight weeks of annual and/or personal leave per year … The Board does not need to be notified."
- Longer than 8 weeks in a year counts as extended leave, which needs advance written approval from Ahpra and can be granted for up to 12 months.
- More than 12 months requires discontinuing the internship and seeking recognition of prior supervised practice.
- Individual supervision must "be provided frequently throughout the internship when practicing (excluding leave periods), regardless of the number of hours per week".
- The logbook is reviewed "weekly, or fortnightly if part-time".

**Telehealth and remote delivery (verified, same source).**
- Client contact "may be in person, by videoconference, or telephone."
- Individual supervision is "conducted either together in same room or through telephone or videoconference".
- Direct observation "may occur live, or via audiovisual recording".

**Work roles (verified, same source).**
- Work roles "must be psychological in nature, either paid or unpaid". More than one role is allowed, and concurrent roles are considered on application.
- "Provisional psychologists are not permitted to work independently or establish an independent private practice." The Board "may approve applications to work in a private practice with other psychologists" with sufficient supervision.
- Teaching, tutoring, supervising others and pure research roles are not accepted. Supervised applied research can count up to a 200-hour cap.
- Work in "another professional field" will not be accepted.

**Starting the internship (verified, same source).**
- Lodge the internship plan (INPP-76) with a position description signed by the workplace manager. The final 5th-year transcript goes directly to Ahpra.
- The internship can start immediately after lodging, and the plan is approved once the degree is completed.
- The intern can keep working in addition to placements for up to 28 days after finishing the 5th year while finalising the plan. Additional fifth-year placements "will not be credited towards the requirements of the 5+1 internship".
- International applicants must have their 5th-year qualification assessed by the Board as substantially equivalent.

**NPE (verified).**
- Fee: $450. — [PsyBA Fees](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Fees.aspx)
- Sittings and mode: [PsyBA NPE page, reviewed 17 Sep 2026](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/National-psychology-exam.aspx)
  - 2026 sittings: 2–20 Feb, 4–22 May, 3–21 Aug, 2–27 Nov.
  - 2027 sittings: 1–19 Feb (registration 8 Dec – 15 Jan), 3–21 May (9 Mar – 16 Apr), 2–20 Aug (8 Jun – 16 Jul), 1–19 Nov (7 Sep – 15 Oct).
  - All four sittings are delivered at a test centre or by online proctoring from home or work.
  - A free practice exam is available.
- Rules: [NPE guidelines, effective 1 Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f34997&dbid=AP&chksum=cTg4k9XkSVP7Kn%2b3tW%2frCw%3d%3d)
  - 5+1 interns are eligible to sit "During the sixth year".
  - The pass mark is a scaled 70%.
  - After a first or second fail, the candidate can re-sit at the next period.
  - After 3 fails, no further sitting until registration renewal or a new application is approved, with a statement and plan (SPPD-76) and conditions on registration.
  - After 6 fails, "the Board may propose to refuse the candidate's application for renewal or registration."
  - Not attending a booked sitting without special consideration counts as a fail.

The guidelines make the arrangement between the intern, the supervisor and the Board (via the internship plan). — [5+1 guidelines (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d)

It notes "some practitioners will offer a discounted rate for supervision of provisional or early career psychologists". — [AAPi Recommended fees 2026–2027 (PDF)](https://aapi.org.au/common/Uploaded%20files/AAPi/Recommended%20fees%20detailed%202026-2027.pdf)
- The Open Arms (DVA) supervisor fee schedule from 1 Jul 2026 reportedly lists a 60-minute professional supervision session at $199.05 including GST (search extract; I did not open the document, and it may be the clinical psychologist schedule). — [Open Arms supervisor fee schedule, 1 Jul 2026 (PDF)](https://www.openarms.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/Open%20Arms%20Supervisor%20Fee%20Schedule%20-%20Psychologist%20and%20MH%20Social%20Worker%201%20July%202026.pdf)
- Funded scholarships cover up to 95 + 13 hours (part-time) or 84 + 12 hours (full-time) of supervision. — [Provisional Psychologist Hub](https://www.provisionalpsych.com.au/supervisionscholarships)

### Inferences
- **Private supervision cost.** At the AAPi recommended rate, 80 hours would cost about $27,200 (80 × $340). This is a ceiling. Employer-provided supervision (common in public health, NGO and school roles) or a Commonwealth scholarship can bring it to $0, and discounted private rates are often lower. No authoritative 2026 market-rate survey was found.
- **Part-time arithmetic.** 1,500 hours at the SPPP's 17–29 h/week band takes about 52–88 weeks of practice, plus leave.
- **Full-time engineering job.** The engineering role cannot count, because the work role must be "psychological in nature". The applicant would need a separate psychological role: part-time alongside the engineering job, or full-time during leave blocks.
- **Leave-block pattern.** Long gaps (more than 8 weeks a year) need extended-leave approval, and supervision must be "frequent" while practising. A pattern of intermittent leave blocks may therefore need Ahpra pre-approval in the internship plan. This is my reading, not a ruling.
- **Telehealth.** Telehealth client contact and remote supervision are permitted. An intern could therefore work in a telehealth service employer, but not as an independent sole trader.

### Gaps
- There is no official guidance on the minimum weekly hours for a part-time 5+1 internship. The 17–29 h/week band is a scholarship eligibility rule, not a Board rule.
- There is no authoritative survey of private supervision fees in 2026.
- Whether Ahpra would approve an internship plan built around intermittent full-time blocks separated by months of non-practice has not been confirmed.

## Q4. Two-year higher-degree pathway: placements, and whether they can be done outside business hours

### Takeaway
APAC requires two-year Level 3+4 coursework masters to include at least 1,000 placement hours across at least 3 placements, typically at least 400 hours of which are direct client work. Professional doctorates need 1,500 hours across at least 4 placements. The first placement must be at least 180 hours.

Only onshore (Australian) placement hours count. A one-year 5th year (MPP, Level 3) needs at least 300 hours of practicum and skills training.

No document says placements can occur outside business hours. That would depend on the host service. Higher-degree graduates do not sit the NPE.

### Cited Findings
**APAC placement minimums (verified).**
- Level 3 and 4 masters (coursework) leading to general registration must "include at least 1000 placement hours comprising supervision hours, direct client and client-related activities, with typically a minimum of 400 of those hours being direct client activities, across a minimum of three placements, or a sound rationale for any variation".
- Professional doctorate: "at least 1500 placement … typically a minimum of 600 … direct client … across a minimum of four placements".
- Stand-alone Level 4 area-of-practice program: at least 750 hours, typically at least 300 direct client hours.
- Level 3 one-year masters (5+1 route): "at least 300 hours of practicum and skills training", which may use simulation.
- Source: [APAC Evidence Guide v1.4, Dec 2024 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Evidence-Guide-v1.4-final.pdf)

**Supervision and onshore rule (verified, same source).**
- The first placement must be "no less than 180 hours", with at least 1 hour of supervision per 7.5 placement hours. Later placements need at least 1 hour per 15 hours.
- "Only placement hours undertaken onshore count towards the requirements for professional registration."

**HISTORICAL baseline (verified).** APAC's 2019 standards (v1.2) list 1,000 placement hours across at least 3 placements for coursework masters and 1,500 across at least 4 for professional doctorates. — [APAC Accreditation Standards v1.2 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/APAC-Accreditation-Standards_v1.2_rebranded.pdf)

**Provisional registration for higher-degree students (verified).**
- Required for the whole degree, including the thesis.
- Doctoral and part-time candidates often need to reapply after 3 years.
- Source: [PsyBA Higher degree FAQ (reviewed 2019, HISTORICAL)](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Higher-Degree-FAQ.aspx); [Provisional standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)

**Program structure (verified).** All placement hours are completed within the degree for full higher-degree pathways. — [APAC Standards v1.2 Appendix (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/APAC-Accreditation-Standards_v1.2_rebranded.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Weekly load.** 1,000 hours over a two-year master's is roughly 2–3 days a week during placement periods. Hosts such as hospitals, community mental health services and university clinics run mainly in business hours.
- **Fit with a 38-hour job.** It is not realistic to combine full placement load with an unchanged 38 h/week job. Unpaid leave blocks could cover a block placement only if the university and host allow intensive or block arrangements. This needs checking with each program.

### Gaps
- No APAC or PsyBA statement on after-hours or weekend placements was found. Practice is set by each university and host.
- I did not confirm whether APAC has published a newer standards version after v1.2 / Evidence Guide v1.4 that changes these hours as of November 2026.

## Q5. General registration vs area of practice endorsement (clinical registrar program), and Medicare Better Access rebates in November 2026

### Takeaway
General registration allows practice as a "psychologist" and billing of Better Access item 80110. Clinical endorsement requires:
- an accredited 5th+6th-year clinical master's, plus
- a 3,000-hour registrar program (88 weeks FTE, 80 hours supervision, 80 hours CPD).

Higher clinical qualifications need fewer registrar hours. 5+1 graduates need a one-year stand-alone bridging Level 4 program (entry requires 12 months of registered practice) plus the registrar program.

Rebates from 1 July 2026 (50+ minute in-person session): $101.55 for a registered psychologist (item 80110) and $149.05 for a clinical psychologist (item 80010). Both are capped at 10 individual sessions per calendar year.

### Cited Findings
**Endorsement routes (verified).** Eligibility for endorsement requires one of:
- (a) an accredited 5th+6th-year area-of-practice qualification plus at least 3,000 hours in the registrar program
- (b) an accredited 6th-year (bridging or stand-alone) qualification plus 3,000 hours
- (c) a 5th+6th-year qualification with a doctoral thesis plus 2,250 hours
- (d) a 5th–7th-year or higher qualification plus 1,500 hours

Bridging/stand-alone programs "are one-year full-time equivalent postgraduate qualifications that enable psychologists with general registration to obtain endorsement upon completion of that qualification and a registrar program."

Registrar table: for a 6th-year qualification, 88 weeks / 3,000 hours / 80 hours supervision / 80 hours CPD. For DPsych: 44 weeks / 1,500 hours / 40 / 40. Supervision is at least 66% individual, at a minimum of 40 hours per FTE year, and may be by video or phone.

Source: [PsyBA Guidelines on area of practice endorsements, effective 1 Dec 2019 (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD19%2f29347%5bv4%5d&dbid=AP&chksum=TRwOc%2fCmNjiKFDtnlUvT%2f1V%2b0r4tj69nxd%2b6fuYS9zg%3d)

**Entry to a stand-alone area-of-practice program (verified).** Applicants without a Level 3 masters need "a minimum of 12 months' practice as a registered psychologist with no conditions" plus an evaluation of prior learning. — [APAC Standards v1.2 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/APAC-Accreditation-Standards_v1.2_rebranded.pdf)

**Fees (verified).** Registrar program approval $303, endorsement application $303, endorsement qualification assessment $388. — [PsyBA Fees](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Fees.aspx)

**Item 80110, registered psychologist (verified).**
- Focussed psychological strategies, at least 50 minutes, in person.
- Schedule fee $119.45, benefit (85%) $101.55, updated 1 Jul 2026.
- Provider must hold "general registration in psychology" and be registered with Services Australia.
- Up to 10 individual services per calendar year, initial course 6.
- Source: [MBS Online item 80110](https://www9.health.gov.au/mbs/fullDisplay.cfm?type=item&q=80110&qt=item)

**Item 80010, clinical psychologist (verified).**
- At least 50 minutes. Schedule fee $175.30, benefit $149.05, updated 1 Jul 2026.
- Provider must hold general registration and be "endorsed by the Board to practise in clinical psychology".
- Referral must come from the patient's MyMedicare practice GP or usual medical practitioner (or a psychiatrist or paediatrician).
- Source: [MBS Online item 80010](https://www9.health.gov.au/mbs/fullDisplay.cfm?type=item&q=80010&qt=item)

**MyMedicare requirement (search extract).** Since 1 November 2025, Better Access benefits are payable only when the plan or referral is from the patient's MyMedicare practice or usual practitioner. — Secondary blogs summarised in search; consistent with the MBS 80010 descriptor above.

**Proposed change (not enacted).** The redesign would make AoPE "a two-year accredited postgraduate program". — [PsyBA consultation report, Sept 2026 (PDF)](https://www.aapi.org.au/common/Uploaded%20files/AAPi/Redesigning-the-psychology-higher-degree-program-Sept-2026.PDF)

### Inferences
- The rebate gap is $47.50 per session (149.05 − 101.55), or about $475 per client per year at the 10-session cap.
- For telehealth private practice, the applicant can bill Better Access as a generalist once generally registered. Clinical endorsement adds the higher rebate but needs roughly 2 more years of registrar supervision after a clinical master's, or bridging plus registrar after 5+1.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the Better Access *telehealth* item numbers and rebates (video/phone equivalents of 80010/80110) for November 2026.
- I did not confirm whether the AoPE guidelines (dated 1 Dec 2019) have been replaced. The Board plans a review in "Phase 5" of its reform (per [AAPi, 29 Sep 2026](https://www.aapi.org.au/Web/Web/About-AAPi/News/Articles/2026/Sept/Psychology_Board_Education_and_Training_Reform_Update.aspx)).

## Q6. Can a provisional psychologist work in paid roles, under what supervision, via telehealth, and can they bill Medicare?

### Takeaway
- **Paid work:** Yes, in approved psychological roles under a Board-approved supervisor. 5+1 interns work under an approved internship plan. Higher-degree students need prior Board approval to work in addition to placements (Form AWOP-76, $0 fee).
- **Telehealth:** Yes. Telehealth client contact and remote supervision are allowed.
- **Independent private practice:** No.
- **Title:** "Provisional psychologist" (or "intern"), never "psychologist".
- **Medicare:** No. Better Access items require general registration.

### Cited Findings
**5+1 interns (verified).** All from the [5+1 guidelines (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD25%2f35126&dbid=AP&chksum=Lp1IB6VqmRgsP7bWS05iGg%3d%3d):
- Work roles may be "paid or unpaid" but must be psychological.
- "Not permitted to work independently or establish an independent private practice". Independent practice is defined as operating as a sole trader, contractor or in a business arrangement with other sole traders receiving fees.
- They must not use the title "psychologist" or "registered psychologist". "Provisional psychologist" or "intern" is allowed.
- Professional indemnity insurance is required.
- Client contact can be in person, by video or by phone.

**Higher-degree students (HISTORICAL FAQ, 2019).**
- Working in addition to placements "must obtain approval from the Board first" via AWOP-76.
- Check that the employer's professional indemnity insurance applies.
- Source: [PsyBA Higher degree FAQ](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Higher-Degree-FAQ.aspx)

**Fee (verified).** The application fee for working in addition to placements is $0. — [PsyBA Fees](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Fees.aspx)

**Paid supervised work (search extract).** The Board's general FAQ notes "it is not unusual for trainees to obtain paid workplace training positions, or further obtain paid work performing duties as a provisional psychologist under supervision on Board approval". — [PsyBA Psychology FAQ](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Standards-and-Guidelines/FAQ/Psychology-FAQ.aspx)

**Medicare (verified).** Better Access 80110 requires the psychologist to hold "general registration in psychology". Item 80010 also requires clinical endorsement. A provisional psychologist therefore cannot bill these items. — [MBS 80110](https://www9.health.gov.au/mbs/fullDisplay.cfm?type=item&q=80110&qt=item); [MBS 80010](https://www9.health.gov.au/mbs/fullDisplay.cfm?type=item&q=80010&qt=item)

### Inferences
- Provisional psychologists can be employed in paid roles that may be delivered by telehealth (e.g. NGOs, EAP providers, school or community services). Income comes from salary, not Medicare billing.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the current text of the "Policy on working in addition to placements". The latest version found is the 2015 revision, per a search extract.

## Q7. Pakistan question: would parallel online study at a Pakistani university earn credit or shorten the Australian pathway?

### Takeaway
Very unlikely to save time or money, and risky.
- PsyBA registration and APAC-accredited program entry are built on *APAC-accredited* sequences. Overseas study counts only if assessed as "comparable" or "substantially equivalent".
- Australian universities decide credit themselves. They usually limit credit to a few equivalent units completed within about 10 years, often only from APAC-accredited programs. Some will not pre-assess domestic applicants before an offer.
- The APS assessment (needed for overseas-trained applicants entering APAC postgraduate programs) can only conclude "three-year" (Level 1) or "four-year" (Level 1+2) comparability, and four-year needs an independent research project.
- PsyBA separately judges "substantial equivalence" when the person applies for registration (overseas assessment fee $718, non-refundable). Pakistan is not on its pre-approved list.

A Pakistani BS Psychology takes about 4 years. An Australian Level 1 + Level 2 sequence for a graduate takes about 2 years full-time (3–4 part-time), so the overseas route does not shorten anything.

### Cited Findings
**PsyBA rules (verified).**
- Provisional registration requires an accredited 4-year sequence completed in the last 10 years, "or a sequence of study that, in the Board's opinion, is substantially equivalent". — [Provisional standard (PDF)](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?record=WD24%2f33838&dbid=AP&chksum=RdWapeP%2f6imKR5k7qKiXEQ%3d%3d)
- The Board pre-recognises only UK BPS-accredited, Ireland PSI-accredited and US APA-accredited qualifications. The page does not address other countries. All overseas-qualified applicants must first apply for provisional registration, assessed against "Board approved assessment criteria". — [PsyBA Meeting the registration requirements (overseas), reviewed 18 Aug 2025](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Overseas-Applicants/registration-requirements.aspx)
- The overseas assessment fee is $718. — [PsyBA Fees](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Fees.aspx)
- It is "non-refundable" even if registration is refused. — [PsyBA Application outcomes, reviewed 2 Dec 2025](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/Registration/Overseas-Applicants/Application-outcomes.aspx)

**APAC rules (verified).** All from [APAC Accreditation Standards v1.2 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/APAC-Accreditation-Standards_v1.2_rebranded.pdf):
- Level 1 entry is "the responsibility of, and at the discretion of, the HEP". "Graduates holding a Bachelor Degree or higher qualification in any other discipline may undertake an APAC-accredited sequence in psychology."
- Level 3 (professional) entry "requires successful completion of an APAC-accredited qualification that achieves foundational competencies and pre-professional competencies. This could be an Australian qualification or an equivalent qualification from an overseas education institution that has been assessed as comparable by the PsyBA when assessing individual applicants for registration."
- Standard 4.2: "Admission and progression requirements and processes are fair, transparent and equitable."

**APAC evidence expectations (verified).** Providers must publish "credit for prior learning rules", and their "approach to recognition of prior learning" is examined at accreditation. I found no numeric APAC-wide cap on credit. — [APAC Evidence Guide v1.4 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Evidence-Guide-v1.4-final.pdf)

**APS assessment (verified).** All from [APS – Assessment for Australian university postgraduate course entry](https://psychology.org.au/about-us/what-we-do/assessments/assessment-overseas-qualifications/australian-university-postgraduate-course):
- "This assessment is required for entry into APAC‑accredited postgraduate psychology programs at Australian universities."
- Possible outcomes: "Three-year comparability" ("meet Level 1: Foundational Competencies only", so an accredited fourth year must be completed in Australia), or "Four-year comparability" ("meet both Level 1 and Level 2").
- Four-year comparability requires evidence of an independent research project.
- "Final decisions regarding admission, credit, and recognition of prior learning are made by individual universities."
- The page does not list the fee or processing time.

**University credit policies (examples):**
- **Cairnmillar Institute (verified).** RPL for non-APAC study "is not normally possible for APAC accredited units except in the instance of overseas qualifications". Overseas studies are evaluated for "strict equivalence", and "the original course will have already been assessed by the APS".
  - Credit is capped at 50% of a program.
  - Credit is "not normally" available for study more than 4 years old, and "cannot be granted" for study more than 10 years old.
  - Thesis credit is only possible in exceptional circumstances, from an APAC-accredited fourth year.
  - Applications are "not normally accepted prior to an offer of admission". For domestic applicants with overseas qualifications, "a pre-offer application will not normally be allowed".
  - Source: [Cairnmillar Advanced Standing and RPL Policy](https://www.cairnmillar.org.au/about/policies/advanced-standing-and-recognition-of-prior-learning-policy/)
- **UTS online Graduate Diploma in Psychology (verified).** Entry is any AQF 7+ qualification "or overseas equivalent". RPL is only for "equivalent subjects from an APAC-accredited Level 1 program in Psychology within the last 10 years", up to 2 intro subjects (12 credit points). Delivery is 100% online, with a minimum duration of 20 months and 6 intakes a year. — [UTS Online GDip Psychology](https://studyonline.uts.edu.au/online-courses/graduate-diploma-psychology)
- **Charles Sturt (HISTORICAL 2019 handbook, search extract).** Credit is for psychology subjects within 10 years "that were part of an APAC accredited university undergraduate program", up to 4 subjects. — [CSU handbook 2019](https://www2.csu.edu.au/handbook/handbook19/courses/GraduateDiplomaofPsychology.html)
- **Deakin GDip Psychology (Advanced) (search extract).** "Recognition of prior learning cannot be granted for studies completed as part of courses that are not APAC-accredited." — [Deakin course page](https://www.deakin.edu.au/course/graduate-diploma-psychology-advanced)

**Pakistani providers (search extracts, low-quality sources).**
- Virtual University of Pakistan is HEC-recognised as an online and distance university. A BS Psychology appears in its program list, but 2026 intake status is unclear. — [Wikipedia (mirror)](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Virtual_University_of_Pakistan); [ProPakistani, 6 Jan 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/01/06/virtual-university-opens-spring-2026-admissions-worldwide/)
- AIOU introduced a BS Psychology in 2025 as a 4-year distance program. — [ProPakistani, 16 Jul 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/07/16/aiou-introduces-three-new-bs-programs/amp/)

**Offshore placements (verified).** Only onshore placement hours count for registration. — [APAC Evidence Guide v1.4 (PDF)](https://apac.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Evidence-Guide-v1.4-final.pdf)

### Inferences
**Who decides what:**

| Decision | Who decides | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Credit or advanced standing inside an Australian accredited course | The Australian university | Under its own RPL policy, constrained by APAC standards and its accreditation |
| Comparability of overseas training, for entry to APAC postgraduate programs | APS | Universities may also require an APS outcome for overseas psychology study at Level 1/2 entry (e.g. Cairnmillar) |
| Substantial equivalence, for registration | PsyBA/Ahpra | Assessed at the provisional registration application |
| Program accreditation and the rules universities must follow | APAC | APAC does not assess individuals' credit |

**Likely value of a Pakistani online BS Psychology:**
- Studying it in parallel with full-time work and Australian study would most likely produce, at best:
  - a few Level 1 units credited, at university discretion, after an offer; or
  - an APS "three-year" outcome, which still leaves the Australian fourth year to do.
- A "four-year" APS outcome would need a research project and Level 2 competencies, which an online Pakistani BS may not demonstrate.
- Even then, the PsyBA would separately judge substantial equivalence when the applicant applies for provisional registration. A university accepting the degree does not bind the Board.

**Time and money:**
- **Time:** No saving. A 4-year Pakistani BS is longer than the Australian 2-year (full-time) graduate route.
- **Money:** Pakistani fees are low, but any saving is likely wiped out by APS assessment fees, the PsyBA overseas assessment ($718), the risk of non-recognition, and duplicated study effort.
- **Overlap rules:** Several Australian graduate diplomas credit only APAC-accredited units, or only 1–2 intro subjects. Many cap the age of credited study at 10 years, and some at 4–7 years.

**Written confirmation to get before paying anything:**
1. A written APS assessment outcome for the specific Pakistani program and transcript.
2. A formal, written credit/advanced-standing assessment from the specific Australian university, listing each unit credited and the version of the course. Note that some providers will not do this before an offer.
3. Ideally, written confirmation from Ahpra/PsyBA on how the overseas qualification would be treated for provisional registration. In practice this happens only through a formal application with the $718 fee.

The Cert IV in Mental Health (VET) is not an APAC-accredited qualification. I found no source indicating it earns credit into accredited psychology units.

### Gaps
- No source specifically on how the APS or PsyBA treats Pakistani psychology degrees, or online/distance degrees, was found.
- I did not find the APS assessment fee or processing time.
- I did not find any Australian university's published credit precedent for VU or AIOU psychology units.
- VU and AIOU program details (curriculum, research project, HEC accreditation status of the specific BS Psychology) came from weak secondary sources and were not verified on vu.edu.pk or aiou.edu.pk.
- I found no APAC-wide numeric rule on credit for prior learning. Limits are set by each provider.
