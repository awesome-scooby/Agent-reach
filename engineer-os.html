Base salary
What every band on this page means unless it says otherwise. Excludes super, car, bonus.
Total remuneration
Base + 12% super + allowances + bonus. A "$250k package" is often a $190–200k base.
Contracting revenue
Rate × hours billed. No leave, no pay between contracts. $120/h is not $250k salary.
Business turnover
Money in. An electrical contractor turning over $1.5m can pay its owner less than a Senior PE.
Owner profit
Turnover minus every cost, before your own tax and super. The only number that compares with a salary.

The $300K challenge

At this level almost nobody is paid a $300k base for doing engineering. The money comes from running a business unit, billing a scarce specialism by the hour, or owning something. Check any day rate here before you believe it.

Day-rate reality check

Costs: professional indemnity and public liability, accounting, software. Weeks worked: 52 minus 4 leave, 2 public holidays, and whatever gap you expect between contracts. Contracting through an agency for one client usually leaves you inside the ATO personal services income rules, so you cannot split the income or hold it in a company .

What the salary numbers are

  • Advertised ranges from job ads and recruiters run high: they describe the top of what an employer will pay.
  • Aggregator averages (Payscale, Employment Hero, Glassdoor) run low or stale: self-reported, mixed seniority.
  • Bands on posters are my synthesis between the two for a competent person in a capital city. Treat them as ±10%.
  • I could not retrieve the role-level tables in the Hays FY25/26 guide or Engineers Australia's salary guide, and I did not open the official JSA shortage spreadsheet. Shortage ratings quoted come from third-party copies of it.
  • Radar entries marked "not verified this pass" rest on general knowledge, not on a source I read today.
