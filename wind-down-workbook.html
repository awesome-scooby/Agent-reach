A quiet workbook for the end of the day

You don't have to do anything with this tonight.

Seven short questions — taps, not typing. About three minutes. Then this becomes a small page you can open on a bad evening and find something that takes two minutes.

What this is not

It isn't therapy, and it won't tell you what's wrong with you. It isn't a habit tracker — there are no streaks, no scores, and nothing to keep up with. Skipping it costs you nothing.

Your answers stay in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no one else can read them — not even the person who sent you this.
